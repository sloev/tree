Gaardsted-Jørgensen Slægt

The ancestry of Johannes Gårdsted Valbjørn, traced through Danish church books and censuses. Your father's line is documented back to the 1700s on Djursland, Mols and in Copenhagen. Your mother's side leads to Egernsund and Broager in Southern Jutland, where Lorenz Heinrich Frederiksen was born in 1932.
