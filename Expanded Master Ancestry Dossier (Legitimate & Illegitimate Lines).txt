I. SCOPE & METHODOLOGICAL NOTE ON NON-TRADITIONAL & ILLICIT LINEAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Genealogical research in Danish parish registers (Kirkebøger), census records 
(Folketællinger), probate records (Skifteprotokoller), and military/adels archives 
frequently reveals complex family dynamics that extend beyond formal nuclear 
marriages. This dossier explicitly incorporates:
1. Legitimate Bloodlines: Standard marital births and generational descents.
2. Illegitimate & Extramarital Bloodlines ("Uægte Børn"): Births recorded out of 
   wedlock where biological paternity is established via church baptismal records, 
   paternity cases (Almendings- og Sognefogedprotokoller), or court documents.
3. Acknowledged Paternity & Social Fatherhood: Instances of step-parenting, 
   fosterage (Plejebørn), and recognized illegitimate children who carry biological 
   bloodlines.
4. Adoptive & Feudal Alliance Ties: Legal and social adoptions, land transfers, 
   and dynastic marriages across Danish, Pomeranian, and German nobility.

II. BRANCH 1: MORFAR'S SIDE – THE GEDDE & VON KLEIST / VON WOYTEN DYNASTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- Subject 1.1: Christina von Woyten ---
* Lineage Status: Legitimate noble birth / Marital alliances.
* Birth: c. 1600 (Hinterpommern, Pomerania).
* Death: After 1645 (Pomerania).
* Locations over time: Versin (Vessin), Bütow, Groß Tychow, Hinterpommern.
* Occupations: Noblewoman (Adel) of the Pomeranian knighthood.
* Parents: Benedictus von Woyten (Captain of Bütow) & Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz.
* Spouses / Marital Connections: 
  - (1) Otto von Below of Pustamin (Widowed prior to 1606).
  - (2) Georg von Kleist of Groß Tychow.
* Children: Hedwig Maria von Kleist (and collateral siblings).
* Books / Art / Music: Feudal land registers and knightly charters.
* Photos / Paintings / Archival Images & Sources:
  - Archival Reference: Zedler, Johann Heinrich. "Grosses vollständiges Universal-Lexicon" (1732–1754), Vol. 59, Article: "Woyden, Woyten".
  - Source URL: https://www.v-kleist.com/FG/Muttrin/Muttrin.pdf

--- Subject 1.2: Georg von Kleist ---
* Lineage Status: Legitimate noble birth.
* Birth: c. 1595 (Groß Tychow, Pomerania).
* Death: After 1645 (Groß Tychow, Pomerania).
* Locations over time: Groß Tychow, Mandelatz, Hinterpommern.
* Occupations: Feudal landowner, Pomeranian nobleman.
* Parents: Joachim von Kleist & Anna von Zitzewitz.
* Spouse: Christina von Woyten.
* Children: Hedwig Maria von Kleist.
* Sources: von Kleist Family Archives (v-kleist.com) Tychow-Dubberow line; https://www.v-kleist.com/FG_allg/Kleist_in_Daenemark.pdf

--- Subject 1.3: Colonel Christian von Kleist ---
* Lineage Status: Legitimate military nobility.
* Birth: c. 1620 (Pomerania).
* Death: Late 17th century.
* Locations over time: Pomerania, Denmark-Norway.
* Occupations: Military Officer (Colonel in Danish/Pomeranian service).
* Spouse: Hedwig Maria von Kleist.
* Children: Christian Adam von Kleist, Christian Casimir von Kleist (b. 1654, d. 1722).
* Sources: H. W. Harbou, "Slægten von Kleist i Danmark," Personalhistorisk Tidsskrift, 3rd Series, Vol. 6 (1897); https://www.v-kleist.com/FG/Muttrin/Muttrin.pdf

--- Subject 1.4: Cartz Ulrik von Kleist ---
* Lineage Status: Legitimate military bloodline.
* Birth: c. 1670.
* Death: c. 1735.
* Locations over time: Pomerania, Schleswig, Denmark.
* Occupations: Military Officer.
* Children: Christian Frederik von Kleist.
* Sources: Harbou (1897); https://www.v-kleist.com/FG/Muttrin/Muttrin.pdf

--- Subject 1.5: Ove Samuel Gedde & Frederikke Louise Dorthea von Kleist ---
* Lineage Status: Legitimate officer marriage connecting the Gedde and von Kleist lines.
* Birth / Death: 18th Century.
* Locations over time: Southern Jutland, Nybøl, Egernsund, Lejrskov.
* Occupations: Military and administrative officers (Gedde officer lineage).
* Children: Successive generations of Danish naval and army officers.
* Sources: Danish National Archives (Rigsarkivet) military rolls; C. E. A. Schøller, "Stamtavle over Officersfamilien Gedde (Gjedde)"; https://slaegtsbibliotek.dk/911651.pdf

III. BRANCH 2: FARMOR LISS' SIDE – VESTJYLLAND (HEDESOGNENE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- Subject 2.1: Jens Peder Larsen (Pårup Melgaard) ---
* Lineage Status: Legitimate agrarian farming lineage with extensive collateral illegitimate/foster connections across heath parishes.
* Birth: 29 February 1812 (Assing Sogn, Ringkøbing Amt).
* Death: 1870 (Sønder Omme, Denmark).
* Locations over time: Pårup Melgaard (Assing), Sønder Felding, Sønder Omme.
* Occupations: Farmer, Landholder (Gårdejer).
* Spouse: Mette Pedersdatter (Married 1843, Sønder Felding Parish).
* Children: Multiple children of the Pårup Melgaard lineage, including documented foster/plejebørn entries in local census records.
* Sources: Horsbøl Genealogical Collection (horsboel.dk/familie/grind/dat16.htm); 1870 Census for Sønder Omme; https://www.horsboel.dk/fkt/FKT_Sdr_Omme_1870.pdf

IV. BRANCH 3: FARFAR'S SIDE – DJURSLAND, MOLS, & COPENHAGEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- Subject 3.1: Niels Jensen (Degnen) & Karoline Dam Jensen ---
* Lineage Status: Legitimate rural lineage intersecting with local parish cottar and schoolmaster bloodlines, noting several historical out-of-wedlock baptisms among extended collateral branches in Kolind and Rosmus.
* Birth: 1857 (Kolind); Death: 1932.
* Spouse: Karoline Dam Jensen (Born: 1863; Died: 1947).
* Parents of William Valbjørn (Born: 1896; Died: 1965) and grandparents of Sigrid Valbjørn (Born: July 15, 1926).
* Locations over time: Kolind, Rosmus, Mols, Copenhagen (Godthåbsvej).
* Occupations: Rural craftsmen, tenant farmers (fæstebønder), school-attendants.
* Sources: Danish Church Books (Kirkebøger) for Kolind and Rosmus parishes via Rigsarkivet; https://sloev.github.io/tree/plakat-A3-fars-side.pdf
