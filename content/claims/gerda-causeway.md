## Claim

Familieerindringen daterer byggestarten til 11. januar 1911 og beskriver en dæmning på 334 meter. Den overordnede historie er omtalt af lokalarkivet, men myndighedsforløb, ansvar og præcis anlægsdato bør dokumenteres.

## Evidence

Familieerindringen daterer byggestarten til 11. januar 1911 og angiver 334 meter. Erling Mortensen, De viste vejen (1969), s. 91, giver uafhængig historisk corroboration: Marius Christensen og familie udførte hovedparten af dæmningsarbejdet i 1911; han overtog Langø i 1894 for 4.000 kr.; dæmningen gik over 530 alen vand og blev anslået til 5.000–10.000 m³ jord. Bogen omtaler desuden en ministeriel resolution efter konflikt med fiskerne. Langeland Byarkivs register til Øboer 2011 bekræfter købet i 1894, 334 m dæmning og landfast forbindelse i 1911.

## Missing

Den ministerielle resolutions dato, myndighed, journalnummer og fulde ordlyd; samtidige aviser; byggeregnskaber eller vej-/landvæsensakter. Den sekundære 1969-bog gengiver ikke selve resolutionen.

## Note: Langø purchase and causeway

Family memoir “En Langø-piges erindringer” says Marius and Ane Marie bought Langø in Lindelse Nor in 1894 for 4,000 kroner, with 1,600 kroner paid down, and moved there with four children. It says Marius and his sons began building the causeway to Kædeby Haver on 11 January 1911; it was eventually 334 metres long and 6–8 metres wide. The memoir also describes earlier grazing use, a prior owner and a forced sale.

Langeland Byarkiv's catalogue independently confirms Lisbeth Skov's 2011 article “Langø og dæmningen”: Marius Christensen and Ane Marie bought Langø in 1894 and built the 334-metre causeway in 1911. The real-register and land-book material for Lindelse and Magleby (1790–2013) is the next route to verify title and transaction terms. The memoir's more detailed financial and personal narrative remains family testimony until checked against the deed, auction and land-book.

## Note: New independent corroboration — Langø's causeway (16 November 2026)

A strong independent printed source has now been located: Erling Mortensen, *De viste vejen: Modtagere af Det kgl. danske Landhusholdningsselskabs sølvbægere 1804–1969* (Landhusholdningsselskabets Forlag, 1969), p. 91. The entry for Marius Christensen, Langø, Humble sogn, says:

- Marius Christensen and his family completed the main part of the causeway work in **1911**; three sons and a son-in-law helped.
- He had taken over Langø in **1894** for **4,000 kroner** (1,600 kroner down payment).
- The causeway crossed about **530 alen of water**, including about 300 alen of soft bottom; the estimated earth volume was **5,000–10,000 m³**.
- The work was described as a long-held dream; the book says a ministerial resolution went against the fishermen after a dispute when Christensen began building.

This is a substantial independent corroboration of the family account's central chronology and identity. It also yields a new archival lead: the **ministerial resolution** is a traceable government decision and may identify the responsible ministry, date, legal basis and any objections from local fishermen. The book is a retrospective secondary source, not the resolution itself; keep the claim open until the original administrative record is found.

A second corroborating route is Langeland Byarkiv's catalogue for Lisbeth Skov's article “Langø og dæmningen” in *Øboer* 2011. Its summary independently states that Marius and Ane Marie Christensen bought Langø in 1894 and that the island was connected to Langeland in 1911 by a 334 m causeway built with help from sons and son-in-law. The full article should be consulted for references and any oral-history details.

Sources:
- Erling Mortensen, *De viste vejen* (1969), p. 91: https://slaegtsbibliotek.dk/2023/935679.pdf
- Langeland Byarkiv, *Øboer* 2011 article register: https://byarkivet.langelandkommune.dk/boeger-brochurer/register-over-artikler-i-oeboer

**Assessment:** 1894 acquisition and 1911 causeway are now strongly supported by independent secondary/local-archive sources. The family recollection's precise start date of 11 January 1911, exact 334 m length and legal/administrative circumstances still need primary-source verification.

## Note: Nyt publiceret spor til dæmningsarbejdet og myndighedskonflikten i 1911

Rudkøbing Byhistoriske Arkivs Øboer-register identificerer Lisbeth Skovs artikel “Langø og dæmningen” (Øboer 2011) og bekræfter i resuméet 1911-anlægget på 334 m udført af Marius Christensen med sønner og svigersøn. En anden publiceret fremstilling om Marius' sølvbæger 1933 beskriver dæmningen som ca. 530 alen, med 5.000–10.000 m³ jord, og fortæller, at fiskerne protesterede, men at en ministeriel resolution gik dem imod. Det styrker eksistensen af en konkret myndighedsafgørelse og giver søgeordene fiskernes indsigelse, Marius Christensen, Langø og dæmningsarbejdet. Det beviser ikke resolutionens dato, myndighed eller journalnummer. Næste trin er at bestille Lisbeth Skovs artikel og følge dens noter til den ministerielle resolution samt søge i relevante ministerielle, amtslige og kommunale journalsager 1910–1912.
