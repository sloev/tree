## Claim

Ove Samuel Geddes identitet rummer flere uløste konflikter: 1778 mod 1788, 28. februar mod 28. maj, og to forskellige Fischer-søstre som mulig mor/stedmor. En hypotese foreslår to mænd med navnet Ove Samuel, men den er ikke bevist.

## Evidence

Harbou angiver 28. februar 1778; en officerbiografi angiver 28. maj 1778; Anker angiver 28. maj 1788. Dossieret beskriver desuden en konflikt mellem den kanoniske moder Øllegaard Sophie Fischer og den foreslåede biologiske mor Frederikke Christiane Fischer.

## Missing

Originale dåbs- og begravelsesindførsler i Fredensborg/Hof- og Slotsmenigheden og Fredrikstad samt vielses-/dødsindførsler, der kan skelne mulige navnebrødre og de to Fischer-søstre.

## Next

Undersøg først dåb og tidlig død for en mulig Ove født 1778; kontrollér derefter Fredrikstad-dåben 1788 og Helsingør-begravelsen 1843, før person eller forældretilknytning ændres.

## Note: Birth date

There is a real date conflict:
- Harbou (1897) gives 28 February 1778–10 March 1843.
- The current tree uses 28 February 1778.
- P.I. Liebe / E. Borgstrøm's officer biography (based on Rigsarkiv military notes) gives 28 May 1778–10 March 1843.
- Carl Johan Anker (1885) gives 28 May 1788; the OCR is marked unproofread and may conflate Ove with his father Hans Christopher.

The year 1778 has stronger support than 1788, but the exact day/month remains unresolved. Inspect the original baptism register in the relevant Fredensborg/Hof- og Slotsmenighed records and compare adjacent entries. Do not silently choose between 28 February and 28 May.

## Note: Fischer sisters and mother conflict

The 2025 expanded research export corrected the proposed parentage: it links Ove (1778) to Hans Christopher Gedde and his first wife **Frederikke Christiane Fischer** (baptised 4 November 1745 at Toten, died 28 February 1787), while **Øllegaard Sophie Fischer** (born 5 August 1752 at Ullensaker, died 9 December 1821) is treated as his stepmother, married to Hans Christopher on 15 December 1789. DBL's Hans Christopher entry distinguishes these as two sisters.

However, the canonical `data/family.json` still links Ove to `p433`, Øllegaard Sophie Fischer, as mother. The expanded research export and canonical tree therefore disagree. This is a priority data-reconciliation issue, but do not change canonical parentage until the original baptism and marriage/death records have been checked.

## Note: Ove Samuel I / II hypothesis

The segment 12 extension argues for two men:
- Ove Samuel I, born 28 February 1778, who died young;
- Ove Samuel II, born 28 May 1788 in Fredrikstad, died 10 March 1843 in Helsingør and buried 16 March at Skt. Mariæ.

It argues that Harbou attached the 1778 birth to the man who married in 1806 and died in 1843. This is a plausible conflation hypothesis, but the 1778 infant's death and the 1788 Fredrikstad baptism have not been inspected. Keep one existing p216 record with the competing dates and maternal possibilities documented; do not create a second Ove or move the canonical FAMC until the Fredensborg/Fredrikstad baptism and burial records are read.

## Note: MyHeritage profile leads: Ove Samuel I / II and sibling candidates (17 November 2026)

- `121.pdf`, p.3: profiles for Friederich Christopher (Frederich) von/Gedde, with variant birth reports 1779 or 1781 and death 1840, list Ove Samuel as a sibling. `121.pdf`, p.10: Nicolaj Wilhelm von Gedde, 1779–1833, is listed with Ove Samuel as a sibling.
- `Alle resultater for gedde.pdf`, p.4: Fredrik Christopher Gedde Fisher, 1781–1840, is listed as Ove Samuel's sibling, with two wives and children shown. The same search snapshot, p.5, gives “Ove Samuel Von Gedde (Født Gedde), Ii”, 1788–1843, and Nicolai Wilhelm Hansen Gedde among the siblings.

These profiles make the existing “I / II” split a concrete lead to test, but do not prove that there were two men. They are user-submitted tree assertions, may copy from one another, and are not independent corroboration. They do not resolve the conflict over 1778/1788, the exact birth date, or the Fischer sisters. Do not create a second person, alter a parent edge, or select one date on this evidence. Next verification remains the original baptism/burial records already specified in this claim.
