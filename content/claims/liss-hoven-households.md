## Claim

Dossieret identificerer en Hoven-husstand omkring 1834 med møller Anders Jensen, enken Ane Kirstine Eskesdatter, Christen Pallesen og Kirsten Marie Jensdatter. Ejendoms- og husholdningssporene kan afklare erhverv og succession, men må ikke bruges alene til at udlede biologisk slægtskab.

## Evidence

Ane Kirstine Eskesdatter (a89) beskrives som enke og ejer af Hoven mølle i 1834. Dossieret angiver også Anders Jensen (a88), Christen Pallesen (a90) og Kirsten Marie Jensdatter (a91) som personer i det lokale materiale.

## Note: 3. Hoven / Sønder Felding line

Hulda Christensen (`p11`) was born 25 September 1895 in Sønder Felding. Her father Mads Christensen/Kristensen (`p22`) was born 18 January 1852 in Hoven and married Karen Jensen in Grindsted in 1875. The Hoven birth entry identifies Mads's parents as Christen Andersen (`a44`) and Kirsten Christensdatter (`a45`).

The Hoven line has useful household evidence: Christen Andersen's mother Ane Kirstine Eskesdatter (`a89`) was a widow and owner of the Hoven mill in the 1834 census. Christen's household in 1860 included several children, including Mads. The mill ownership and widow household are useful collateral evidence for identity and chronology, but should not be treated as a substitute for the birth and marriage entries.

## Note: Hoven mill

The Hoven line is tractable because the mill was a fixed property. The extension identifies:
- Anders Jensen (`a88`), miller in Hoven, died before 1834;
- Ane Kirstine Eskesdatter (`a89`), widow and owner of the Hoven mill in 1834;
- Christen Pallesen (`a90`), farmer in Hoven, died before 1834;
- Kirsten Marie Jensdatter (`a91`), farmer's widow in 1834 and aftægtskone 1840–1860.

Next checks are Hoven baptisms 1770–1800 and marriages c.1770–1785, the Hoven mill deed and fire-insurance protocol, the 1834 matrikel, and a full reading of 1834 census household 124565. A mill deed/insurance record may establish property succession and occupation, but it does not replace the parish evidence for parentage.
