# The 1788 year is incompatible with Ove Samuel's documented maternal line

Dansk Biografisk Leksikon, “H.C. Gedde”, family section, states that Hans Christopher Gedde's first wife **Frederikke Christiane Fischer died on 28 February 1787 in Fredrikstad**. His second marriage to her sister Øllegaard Sophie Fischer did not take place until **15 December 1789**.

The 1843 *Fædrenelandsk Nekrolog* identifies Ove Samuel's mother as Frederikke, née Fischer, and gives his birth as **28 May 1778**. The obituary was published in the year of Ove's death and is the strongest near-contemporary biographical source in this conflict.

This has a clear consequence: if the obituary's maternal identification is correct, Ove cannot have been born in 1788. His mother had been dead for over a year, and his father's second marriage was still in the future. Thus Anker (1885) and *Norske Slægter* (1912), which give 28 May 1788, are incompatible with the documented parentage. **The year 1778 is strongly preferred.**

## What remains unresolved

- *Fædrenelandsk Nekrolog* (1843) and Liebe/Borgstrøm support **28 May 1778**.
- Harbou/PHT (1897) gives **28 February 1778**.
- The canonical public person record p216 remains **1778-02-28** until the original Fredrikstad baptism record is inspected.
- The exact Fredrikstad parish register series is Ministerialbok nr.2 (1750–1804; Arkivportalen catalogue years 1753–1804). Search the baptism entries for 1777–1779 under Ove, Ove Samuel, Ove Samuël, Gedde/Gjedde, and father Hans Christopher/Hans Christoffer. If absent, test nearby garrison parishes.

**Disposition:** reject 1788 under the current maternal identification; retain 1778 as the birth year; do not choose 28 May over 28 February without the original register.
