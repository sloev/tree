## Claim

Et skifte fra 1820 omtaler Maren Poulsdatter, hustru til en Hans Jensen, gartner ved Vibygård, og fire voksne børn. Det er ikke fastslået, at denne Hans Jensen er samme person som den kanoniske Hans Jensen (a66), der i 1822 blev gift med Margrethe Pedersdatter.

## Evidence

Dossieret skelner udtrykkeligt mellem 1820-skiftet og Hans Jensen (a66), som folketællingen 1834 beskriver som aftægtsmand og tidligere gartner ved Wiibygaard.

## Missing

Originalt skifte med afdødes identitet, hustru, børnenes navne, alder, bopæl og eventuelle værger; sammenhold med vielsen 1822 og folketællingen 1834.

## Next

Find og læs originalskiftet fra 1820. Hold de to husstande adskilt, medmindre dokumentet giver en entydig identifikation.

## Note: Canonical ancestor: Hans Jensen (`a66`)

The current tree identifies Hans Jensen, former gardener at Vibygård, married Margrethe Pedersdatter in Syv in 1822. Their daughter Johanne Sophie Hansen was born in Syv in 1825. The 1834 census says Hans was a retired gardener at “Wiibygaard” and living on aftægt in Syv; he died in 1837. This explains why Johanne Sophie is sometimes recorded as born at Viby.

## Note: Separate 1820 probate lead

A separate probate extract reportedly names **Maren Poulsdatter**, wife of Hans Jensen, gardener at Vibygård, and four adult children (Poul, Jens, Peder and Ane). This is not automatically the same Hans Jensen as `a66`, whose wife in the current tree is Margrethe Pedersdatter and whose marriage is in 1822. Keep the households separate until the original probate identifies the gardener, spouse, children and dates.

## Note: 10. Additional Vibygaard probate detail — separate household pending identity

The 1820 Vibygaard Gods probate is cited as book 2, folio 21 (with related folios 22 and 32–34), dated 31 December 1820. The extension transcription identifies the deceased as **Maren Poulsdatter**, wife of a gardener Hans Jensen, and names four adult children:

- Poul Hansen — smallholder in Assingløse;
- Jens Hansen — gardener;
- Peder Hansen — smallholder in Viby;
- Ane Hansdatter — married to Willum Hansen, farmer in Valsø.

The same extension proposes that the husband was existing ancestor `a66`, Hans Jensen, former gardener at Vibygård, but the review correctly held that identity unresolved pending the original 1822 Syv marriage. The family structure within the 1820 probate may be sound while the identification with `a66` is not. Keep these four people and the 1820 widow in this dossier as an **unlinked Vibygaard household** until the 1820 original and 1822 Syv marriage have been compared by age, occupation, spouse, children and residence.
