## Claim

Claus Gaardsteds skifte og en mulig auktion over Bogensholm i 1811 kan afklare hans husstand, første ægteskab og børn samt ejendommens overgang. De mulige forbindelser er endnu ikke dokumenteret i en sammenhængende originalkildekæde.

## Evidence

Evald-dossieret udpeger 1811-skiftet og auktionen som konkrete arkivspor. Det hidtidige materiale identificerer ikke sikkert første hustru eller eventuelle børn.

## Missing

Originalt skifte, auktions-/skødeprotokol og præcis arkivskaber, dato og parternes navne.

## Note: Claus Gaardsted (`p72`) and Karen Christensdatter

**Status: strong sibling clue; parentage unresolved.**

The Rolsøgård G302 alphabetical probate index, under Niels Jensen Degn in Vrinners, probate no. 11, 14 May 1787, folio 39, records:

> “Arv i boet til afdødes stedbørn efter Christen Christensen Tohde? foged i Dejret (skifte 1764) til datter Karen Christensdatter LV: broder Claus Gaardsted forpagter på Vosnæsgård.”

The index identifies Karen Christensdatter as sister of Claus Gaardsted, tenant at Vosnæsgaard, and says he acted as her lavværge (legal guardian). It points to an earlier 1764 probate for bailiff Christen Christensen “Tohde?” in Dejret.

**Do not infer** that Tohde was Claus's or Karen's biological father. The wording refers to stepchildren and is not sufficiently clear to establish biological parentage. The original 1764 probate must be located and read. Also inspect the original 1787 folio 39 rather than relying solely on the alphabetical index.

Source: [Rolsøgård G302 index, 1778–1816](https://slaegt.dk/media/jnnnvuml/alfabetisk-navneliste-og-skifteuddrag-til-rols%C3%B8g%C3%A5rd-gods-g302-mols-herred-1778-1816-1818.pdf).

## Note: Primary archive series located for the 1811 probate/auction search

DAISY lists a name register to the probate protocol (1794–1917), probate designations/death notifications (1795–1851), probate protocol for Sønder Djurs and Mols (1794–1824), auction protocol (1792–1814), a wider auction protocol (1753–1928), auction case files (1791–1928), and a justitsprotokol (1688–1919) with the 1803–1814 subseries. Rigsarkivet's Arkivalieronline lists the justitsprotokol as scanned and viewable online. The published G298 estate extract previously checked does not list Claus Gaardsted; this is a jurisdiction-level alternative, not evidence that a Claus file has been found. Search the probate name register and death notifications around 1811 first, then follow the entry into the 1794–1824 probate protocol; search the 1792–1814 auction protocol and auction case files separately for the widow's 1811 sale.
