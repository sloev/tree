# Three concrete results for the St. Croix investigation

## 1. The January 12, 1922 newspaper text reads GEDDE

*The Herald*, Thursday 12 January 1922, Vol. VII, no.55, PDF text layer at the lost-property notice for two silver forks reads:

**“MR. GEDDE, Slob Experimental Station.”**

The forks were lost on the road between Diamond School and Kingshill. This is a real contemporary placement of a person with surname Gedde at Slob in January 1922. A different OCR rendition of the 6 February 1922 issue reads “CEDDE”; that variant must not be confused with the 12 January issue. The January issue's forename is not supplied, and the page image still deserves visual inspection. The contact person is therefore not yet proved to be Emil Ludvig Eduard Gedde, even though the timing agrees with the 1944 biographical entry.

PDF: https://ufdcimages.uflib.ufl.edu/AA/00/04/07/58/00284/AA00040758_00284.pdf

## 2. The 1944 biographical directory explicitly gives Gedde the Danish Club chairmanship

*Danske Landbrugskandidater* (1944), printed p.75, no.563, identifies Emil Ludvig Eduard Gedde (born 15 November 1875; landbrugskandidat 1896) and reports:
- station leader on St. Croix, 1915–1922;
- chairman of St. Croix danske Klub og Læseforening, 1919–1922;
- plantation owner in Argentina from 1923;
- chairman of the Eldorado sickness fund, 1926–1931;
- vice-president of the Lutheran congregation in Eldorado, 1931.

This is a direct statement in a professional biographical directory, whose preface says office-holding and career information was collected through questionnaires. It is strong biographical evidence, not the club's own minute book. A later *St. Croix Avis* issue (6 July 1925) documents the association still operating and names Enar Svendsen as chairman at that later date:
https://ufdcimages.uflib.ufl.edu/AA/00/08/36/39/04083/1925070601.pdf

No 1919–1922 board minute or election notice has yet been located. Royal Danish Library confirms that *The Herald* (1915–1925) is digitised:
https://www.kb.dk/inspiration/aviser-fra-dansk-vestindien

## 3. The exact Danish archive target is series 904, one package

Rigsarkivet's *Vejledende Arkivregistraturer XX: Koloniernes Centralbestyrelse* (1975), printed p.80, identifies “Landbrugsforsøgsstationen på Skt. Croix” as **series no. 904**, covering **1907–1917**, comprising **one package**. The archive creator is Koloniernes Centralbestyrelse, Vestindisk Kontor. Arkivalieronline lists the series under “Plantager (Vestindien)”:
https://arkivalieronline.rigsarkivet.dk/da/other/other-collection/110

The exact package contents have not been read. Request package 904 and associated journal files for Gedde/Gjedde/Eduard/Emil, appointment, pay, instructions, housing and 1917 handover.

## What the USDA conflict means

The official USDA sources do not support a simple story in which Gedde was necessarily the station's top agronomist. Longfield Smith appears in 1918 and in the 1920 annual report as “Agronomist in Charge”; *Experiment Station Record* directories list J. B. Thompson as “Agronomist in charge” in 1920 and 1922; the 1923 annual report lists Thompson. This is an unresolved contradiction/overlap among official staff records. Gedde's 1918 signature and 1922 Slob contact prove a local operational connection, but not his exact formal rank.

## Disposition

The 1919–1922 chairmanship is now supported by a specific printed biographical statement. The January 1922 notice is correctly transcribed as GEDDE for that issue. The Danish archive search is narrowed to one package, no.904. Do not upgrade Gedde to station director or add an exact appointment until the package or U.S. personnel records are read. No family relationships or canonical dates are changed.
