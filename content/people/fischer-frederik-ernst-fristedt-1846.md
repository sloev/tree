Arends identifies him as a son in the Fischer branch descending from Caroline Frideriche Wilhelmine Gedde through the Rev. Ernst Christian Fischer (1806–1878). He attended Odense Latinskole, left his studies, and went to the Cape Colony as a young man, where he worked in the diamond mines at Kimberley. Arends says he probably died there and was probably unmarried. No exact departure, arrival, death date, or primary emigration record has yet been located.
