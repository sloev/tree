## What the image shows

The Argentina section of *De Danskes Vej: Danske pionerer og dansk virke under alle himmelstrøg*, vol. III, places two portrait captions on printed page 79:

- "Plantageejer Karl Schroder, Eldorado"
- **"Plantageejer Eduard Gedde, Eldorado"**

The following text on printed page 80 identifies Edouard Gedde among the agricultural-college graduates counted among Eldorado's pioneers. The OCR text and caption are visible at the source's printed pp.79–80 (PDF text around the page break following p.78).

## Evidentiary value

This is a named portrait, not merely a generic photograph of Eldorado. It is direct evidence that the publication presented an individual called Eduard Gedde as a plantation owner in Eldorado. It strengthens the identification of the Argentina-based Eduard/Eduard(o) Gedde, but does not by itself prove that he is identical with Emil Ludvig Eduard Gedde (born 15 November 1875) or resolve the conflicting 1876–1951 tree dates. Compare with the 1944 *Danske Landbrugskandidater* entry and the 1954 death register.
