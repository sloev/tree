# A second contemporary USDA record names J. B. Thompson at St. Croix

The personnel directory printed in *Experiment Station Record*, vol. 43 (July–December 1920), p. II, lists:

**VIRGIN ISLANDS — St. Croix: J. B. Thompson**

The page's legend identifies footnote 2 as “Agronomist in charge.” The same name and place recur in *Experiment Station Record*, vol. 46 (January–June 1922), p. II, again with footnote 2, “Agronomist in charge.”

This conflicts directly with the *Report of the Virgin Islands Agricultural Experiment Station, 1920*, signed at St. Croix on 13 August 1920 by Longfield Smith as “Agronomist in Charge.” Both are official USDA-era sources. The contradiction must remain visible rather than be resolved by choosing one document.

## What can safely be said

- Longfield Smith was described as Director of the St. Croix Agricultural Experiment Station in Sydney Harland's memoir of his 1913–1914 employment there; he was also named “Agronomist in Charge” in the 1918 federal station directory and in the 1920 annual report.
- J. B. Thompson was listed at St. Croix as “Agronomist in charge” in the July–December 1920 and January–June 1922 *Experiment Station Record* directories.
- The 1923 annual report's station staff lists John B. Thompson as “Agronomist in Charge”.
- The 1927 USDA publication catalogue attributes the 1919–1921 annual reports to Longfield Smith (with C. E. Wilson from 1920), and the 1922 report to J. B. Thompson and C. E. Wilson.

These records may reflect a changeover, overlapping appointments, different administrative/reporting roles, or a mistake in one of the lists. The sources do not currently establish which explanation is correct.

## Implication for Gedde

The 1918 *The Herald* notice signed “EDUARD GEDDE” remains evidence of a real operational connection to Slob Experimental Station. But neither the 1918/1920 Smith sources nor the 1920/1922 Thompson directories establish Gedde's exact title. The 1944 Danish biography's phrase “Forsøgsleder paa St. Croix 1915–1922” should therefore remain a secondary biographical assertion pending appointment, payroll, or station correspondence records.
