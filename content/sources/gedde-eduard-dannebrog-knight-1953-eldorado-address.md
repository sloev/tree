## Extract

In *De Kongelige Danske Ridderordener og Medailler*, biographical section for Knights of the Order of the Dannebrog, printed p.99, the entry reads:

**“Gedde, Eduard (R. 2/5 1953), plantageejer. Adr. Eldorado, Misiones, Argentina.”**

The abbreviation “R.” appears in the register's award/decoration format; the date is 2 May 1953. The entry explicitly locates Eduard Gedde as a plantation owner at Eldorado, Misiones, Argentina, in a volume published in 1954.

## Evidentiary value

This is a second Danish printed source, independent of *De Danskes Vej*, that identifies Eduard Gedde as a plantation owner resident at Eldorado. It also indicates a Danish royal honour dated 2 May 1953. It strongly supports the existence of a Danish Eduard Gedde in Eldorado in 1953, but does not alone establish his full identity as Emil Ludvig Eduard Gedde (born 15 November 1875). Compare the 1944 professional directory, the named portrait in *De Danskes Vej*, and the 1954 death register.
