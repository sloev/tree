## Relevance

This is the published finding aid used to compare the Karen Christensdatter references in the Evald line. It records a likely continuity between the 1772 and 1774 references to a widow after Erik Hvol. The 1775 entry independently within the same compilation identifies a daughter Karen of Kirsten Jensdatter and bailiff Christen Christensen, living with stepfather Niels Jensen in Vrinners. These details are compatible with the later Rolsøgård G302 entry, but do not by themselves establish that the women are the same person.

The original Isgård G299 register and probate protocol must be read before treating any identity or family relationship as established.
