## Relevance

This is a geographically matched contemporary record series for the Magleby–Tryggelev midwife district. It is a promising route to the reported birth of Christen (Kresten) Henriksen Jørgensen on 3 November 1877 in Søndenbro, Magleby. Because the catalogue lists both 1872–1877 and 1877–1883, both must be checked; the year overlap does not establish which volume contains 3 November.
