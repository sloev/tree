## Relevance

The guide explains why the Magleby–Tryggelev midwife protocol is a strong next source to inspect: if the 3 November 1877 birth was entered and the relevant volume survives, it may record the mother's full name and marital status, alongside birth and locality details. This describes the expected record type, not the contents of Kresten's entry.
