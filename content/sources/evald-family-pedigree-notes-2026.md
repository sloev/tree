## Description

Two five-column handwritten pedigree charts preserved within the family. The charts cover a Gaardsted/Andersen line centred on Tirstrup, Rosmus and Fuglslev in Djursland, and a Jørgensen/Larsson line connecting Zealand and Skåne. The original photographs and full working transcriptions remain in the private family research archive and are not reproduced on this public site.

The charts are treated as trusted family-source testimony about the relationships recorded by their author. Their statements are preserved as such; they are not silently replaced by conflicting online trees or by discrepancies in the current GEDCOM.

## Reading and limitations

Both photographs were visually reviewed across the overview and all nine detail tiles. A separate diplomatic transcription and normalized reading were prepared. The second chart's generation columns were rechecked: Jørgen Anton Jørgensen and Lovisa Larsson are the parent couple; Niels Jørgensen and Johanne Sofie Hansdatter, and Jöns Larsson and Anna Marie Jönsdotter, are the two grandparent couples.

Some handwritten dates and names remain incomplete or uncertain. The charts are family pedigree sources, not civil or church registers; this source establishes what the family notes record, not independent documentary verification of every event or biological relationship.
