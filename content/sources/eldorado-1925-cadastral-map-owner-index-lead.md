## Catalogue description

The official historical archive of Eldorado describes its 1925 *Plano de la Colonia Eldorado* as a cadastral plan at 1:30,000 scale covering 21,038 hectares. It contains **413 numbered rural lots**, each showing its number, surface area and **owner's surname**. The archive also describes later plans from 1930, 1938, 1939 and 1945, which continue to identify owners by surname.

Official archive page: https://historia.eldorado.gob.ar/planos-historicos

## Why this matters for the Gedde lead

The 1925 and later cadastral plans offer a direct route to test whether Eduard Gedde held land in the colony and to locate the parcel geographically. The 1925 plan is particularly close to the 1923 date when *Danske Landbrugskandidater* says he became a plantation owner in Argentina.

**Important limitation:** the map has not yet been visually inspected at sufficient resolution to confirm a GEDDE-labelled parcel. This is an identified research target, not proof that a Gedde lot exists. The official page says the 1925 plan was supplied for reproduction and publication by engineer Jorge Antonio Kirchner.
