# Personnel chronology hidden in the USDA publication catalogue

The USDA's *List of Publications ... January 1901 to December 1925*, compiled by Mabel G. Hunt and published in 1927, gives the following annual-report authors for the Virgin Islands Experiment Station (printed p. 167):

| Report year | Author(s) | Publication year |
|---|---|---|
| 1919 | Longfield Smith | 1920 |
| 1920 | Longfield Smith and C. E. Wilson | 1921 |
| 1921 | Longfield Smith and C. E. Wilson | 1922 |
| 1922 | J. B. Thompson and C. E. Wilson | 1923 |
| 1923 | J. B. Thompson and W. M. Perry | 1924 |

This establishes a clear official reporting transition: Longfield Smith is named for all three report years 1919–1921; J. B. Thompson replaces him in the 1922 report year. The 1920 report's title page, staff list and letter of transmittal separately identify Smith as “Agronomist in Charge” in August 1920.

## Implication for Emil Ludvig Eduard Gedde

*Danske Landbrugskandidater* (1944), p.75 no.563, reports that Gedde was “Forsøgsleder paa St. Croix” from 1915 to 1922. The contemporaneous *The Herald* notice in April 1918, signed “EDUARD GEDDE”, proves that a person using that name had an operational connection to Slob Experimental Station. A January 1922 notice places “Mr. Gedde” at Slob, although the OCR reading of the surname needs image verification.

The USDA chronology does **not** prove that Gedde was absent or uninvolved. It does establish that he was not the named author of the official Virgin Islands station annual reports for 1919–1921, and that the official agronomist-in-charge role was held by Longfield Smith in 1920. By report year 1922, J. B. Thompson had taken over the annual report. Gedde may have had a distinct local, administrative, field, estate or Danish-club role; the 1944 title may also compress different periods/functions. These are hypotheses, not conclusions.
