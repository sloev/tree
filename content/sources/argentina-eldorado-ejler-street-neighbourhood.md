## 1. Calle Ejler Gedde in a 2000 international bulletin

The Catholic Biblical Federation's English-language bulletin (issue 55/56, 2000), printed p. 18, gives the address of Centro Bíblico “Dabar” as “Ejler Gedde 145, 3380 Eldorado km 6 (Misiones)”. This is evidence that a street named Ejler Gedde was in use as an address by 2000.

Source: https://c-b-f.org/Documents/cbf-bulletin/Printed-Bulletin-Archive/BDV-English/2000_2-3_-55-56-_EN_BDV.pdf

## 2. Barrio Gedde by 2004

A report in El Territorio dated 26 November 2004 locates an incident near the pedestrian bridge of “barrio Gedde”, about 1,000 metres north of the entrance to Eldorado. This independently shows the neighbourhood name in local use by 2004.

Source: https://www.elterritorio.com.ar/noticias/2004/11/26/22557-debido-a-las-picadas-transito-intensificara-los-controles

## 3. The named streets and the property corner

A local report summarising the Eldorado Deliberative Council's 26 August 2021 session refers to Barrio Gedde and describes Calle Salto F. Peixoto between Salto Tres Mosqueteros and Avenida Formosa. The 2023 official court notice concerning Ejler Gedde's former property places it at the corner of Avenida Formosa and Salto F. Peixoto. These references locate the property in the same neighbourhood context.

Source: https://www.stopenlinea.com.ar/noticias/2021/08/27/63290-resumen-de-la-sesion-ordinaria-del-jueves-26-de-agosto

## 4. Interpretation and limits

Taken together, the records establish that Calle Ejler Gedde was an address by 2000, Barrio Gedde was named in local reporting by 2004, and the property named in the 2023 court notice lies at a street corner that local reporting places in Barrio Gedde.

This is a notable local-memory/toponymic association for the Gedde research. **It does not, by itself, prove when or why the street or neighbourhood received the name, nor that it was named specifically after the Ejler Gedde in the court notice.** The municipal naming ordinance or original street-plan resolution remains an important target.
