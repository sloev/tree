## Relevance to the Kresten/Marthe question

These are three distinct follow-up routes in the correct local jurisdiction:

1. **Alimentation:** Search the *Navneregister til alimentationsjournal* (1867–1917) for Lars Thomsen Larsen, Marthe Jørgensen / Marthe Christine Jørgensen, and spelling variants; then inspect any corresponding entry in the *Alimentationsjournal* (1857–1917). A case could document a maintenance claim or proceedings concerning an alleged non-marital child. The catalogue alone does not show that such a case exists.
2. **Death notice:** Search the *Navneregister til dødsanmeldelsesprotokol* (1875–1979) for Marthe Jørgensen / Marthe Christine Jørgensen around August 1878; inspect the corresponding *Dødsanmeldelsesprotokol* (1856–1919). This may provide an independent administrative trace of the woman indexed as dying in Søndenbro on 27 August 1878. It is not a substitute for the parish burial entry and may not identify her parents or child.
