## Relevance

This is the separate published source for the 1787 reference to Karen Christensdatter and her brother Claus Gaardsted in the estate of Niels Jensen Degn. It provides the later cross-reference to a 1764 probate after a foged in Dejret, but the original wording, punctuation and legal relationships remain unverified until folio 39 is inspected.
