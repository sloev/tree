## Relevance

The entry supplies a concrete identification lead for the foged/ladefoged named in the 1764 and 1787 probate references: a Christen Christensen Gårdsted was recorded at Dejret in 1750 and described as formerly a bailiff at Rolsøgård. This is a strong name-place-occupation match, but it remains an identity hypothesis until the original lease record and probate are compared.
