## 1. Eduardo Gedde at the Martín Lutero school foundation, May 1932

A local history by Frances Lowe, published 29 August 2021, reports that at the laying of the foundation stone for the Lutheran school at km 6 in May 1932, Eduardo Gedde spoke in Spanish on behalf of Eldorado's Evangelical community and thanked the congregation for its support.

The article cites the 1974 institutional history *Congregación Evangélica San Juan, Congregación Martín Lutero*, p. 10, for the early school history. The 1974 book is the next primary/near-primary source to obtain.

Source: https://www.noticiasdelacalle.com.ar/amp/noticias/2021/08/29/56627-el-colegio-martin-lutero-km-6--escuela-luterana

## 2. Eduardo Gedde among CEEL's earliest members (1951)

Two local histories report that the Cooperativa de Electricidad de Eldorado Ltda. (CEEL) was founded on 5 August 1951 and had 179 members at the close of its first financial year. The member-roll copy described below lists GEDDE EDUARDO as member no. 64, with district code A, registered on 26 September 1951 — only 52 days after the cooperative's founding and within its first 179 recorded members. This is strong evidence of early participation in Eldorado's new cooperative, but it does not make him a founder or establish a family relationship.

Founding-history references:
- MisionesOnline, 10 January 2005: https://misionesonline.net/2005/01/10/la-renovacion-desplazo-al-pj-en-la-conduccion-de-la-ceel-de-eldorado/

### CEEL 2012 member-roll copy

A document titled *Padrón 2012 - CEEL*, uploaded to Yumpu on 27 November 2012, lists the entry:

- member number: 64
- name: GEDDE EDUARDO
- district code: A
- registration/alta date: 26/09/1951
- address field: blank/zero in the displayed row

The table headings are “Socio”, “Razón”, “Distrito”, “Dirección”, …, “fec_alta”. In the row, the A after GEDDE EDUARDO occupies the Distrito column; it is not evidence of a middle initial. The date 26/09/1951 is the registration date, not a birth date. The copy is hosted by Yumpu rather than CEEL's own site; the original roll and member application must be requested from CEEL before treating the entry as a primary-source transcription.

## 3. Eduardo and Ejler in the Danish-colony cemetery history

The centenary history of Club Argentino Danés “La Esperanza”, published by Primera Edición on 29 June 2022, states that Eduardo Gedde received the distinction “Ridder of Danebrog”, was responsible for the Danish cemetery and was later replaced by Ejler Gedde.

Source: https://www.primeraedicion.com.ar/nota/100609441/100-anos-del-club-argentino-danes-la-esperanza/

## 4. Link to the older Danish pioneer references

*De Danskes Vej*, vol. III, printed p. 80, includes the caption “Plantageejer Eduard Gedde, Eldorado” and names “Landbrugskandidaterne Edouard Gedde og Karl Schrøder” among Eldorado's pioneers. The similarity of Eduard, Edouard and Eduardo is a search clue, not proof that all references concern one man.

Source: https://slaegtsbibliotek.dk/910949.pdf

## What this establishes — and what it does not

The records establish a man named Eduardo Gedde as an active public figure in Eldorado's Evangelical community in 1932, a person named Eduardo Gedde in a reproduced CEEL member roll with registration date 26 September 1951, and an Eduardo Gedde who preceded Ejler Gedde in the Danish cemetery role according to the club's later history.

They do **not** yet establish that these three Eduardo references are one person, that Eduardo A. is the Edouard in the Danish book, or that Eduardo was Ejler's father. The transition of cemetery responsibility may be familial, but it could also be an institutional succession.

## Highest-value follow-up

Request from CEEL the original membership ledger and application file for member no. 64, GEDDE EDUARDO (district A), registered 26 September 1951. Ask for the original member card, date of birth, full name, address at registration, occupation, signature, land/property references and any linked household or successor records. Do not publish any living-person identifiers if the file includes them.

Also request the 1974 Lutheran congregation history (p.10) and church/cemetery records for Eduardo Gedde and Ejler Gedde, including appointment dates, burial registers, family inscriptions and obituaries.


## 1944 biographical directory: identity and career now strongly established

*Danske Landbrugskandidater* (1944), trykt s.75, nr.563, identifies **Emil Ludvig Eduard Gedde**, born 15 November 1875 at Tyrstinggaard near Brædstrup. It records him as a landbrugskandidat from 1896, experimental-station director on St. Croix 1915–1922, chairman of the St. Croix Danish Club and Reading Association 1919–1922, plantation owner in Argentina from 1923, chairman of Eldorado's sickness fund 1926–1931, and vice-president of the Lutheran congregation in Eldorado in 1931; address Puerto Eldorado, Misiones, Argentina. Source: https://slaegtsbibliotek.dk/929223.pdf.

This is a strong biographical match to the “Eduard/Edouard Gedde” in *De Danskes Vej*, vol. III, p.80. His 1931 Lutheran-congregation office also makes it very likely that he is the Eduardo Gedde who spoke at the Martín Lutero school foundation-stone ceremony in May 1932. The congregation's 1974 history and contemporary minutes should still be checked before calling the 1932 identity fully proven. The 1951 CEEL member no.64 may be the same man but needs the original card. No father-child link to Ejler is established.
