SECTION I: INTRODUCTION & FAMILY BRANCHES OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This dossier compiles the genealogical records of the user's family tree, 
encompassing four primary ancestral lines as documented in church books, 
censuses, local archives, and family papers:
1. Farfar's Side (Djursland, Mols, and Copenhagen): 18th-20th century agrarian and urban families.
2. Farmor Liss' Side (Vestjylland / Heath parishes - Assing, Sønder Felding, Sønder Omme): 18th-19th century pioneers and farming families.
3. Morfar's Side (Nybøl, Egernsund, Lejrskov, Officersslægten Gedde, and Pomeranian noble house von Kleist / von Woyten): Military officers, nobility, and Southern Jutland lineages.
4. Mormor Gerda's Side (South Langeland and the Langø family): Coastal and agricultural lineages of Langeland.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- Subject 1.1: Christina von Woyten ---
* Birth: c. 1600 (Hinterpommern, Pomerania)
* Death: After 1645 (Pomerania)
* Locations: Versin (Vessin), Bütow, Groß Tychow, Hinterpommern.
* Occupations: Noblewoman (Adel) of the Pomeranian knighthood.
* Parents: Benedictus von Woyten (Captain of Bütow) & Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz.
* Spouses: (1) Otto von Below of Pustamin; (2) Georg von Kleist of Groß Tychow.
* Children: Hedwig Maria von Kleist (and others).
* Books / Art / Music: None recorded; subjects of Pomeranian feudal land registers.
* Photos / Paintings / Archival Images: 
  - Archival Document Reference: Zedler Universal Lexicon (1732–1754), Article: "Woyden, Woyten".
