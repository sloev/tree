# Genealogical research ledger

**Purpose:** a source-aware working ledger for expanding the Gårdsted / Gedde family network. This includes biological descent (whether marital or non-marital), marriage and partnerships, adoption, step-parenthood, fosterage, guardianship, acknowledged/legal/social parenthood and other well-founded connections.

**Evidence rule:** preserve a claim when it is historically meaningful, but never promote it beyond what its source establishes. A record that a person declared paternity is evidence of the declaration and its legal/administrative consequences; it is not automatically proof of biological paternity.

## 1. Verified von Woyten / von Kleist additions

The English translation of the von Kleist family history, *History of the Family von Kleist – Tychow-Dubberow line*, p. 123, states that:

- Georg von Kleist of Groß-Tychow (died after 1645) was married to Christina von Woyten.
- Christina was the widow of Otto von Below of Pustamin before marrying Georg.
- Christina was the daughter of Benedictus von Woyten of Versin, princely Pomeranian captain of Bütow, and Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz.
- Georg and Christina had one daughter, Hedwig Maria, who married Colonel Christian von Kleist (II. 60).
- Georg's feudal estates passed to his son-in-law Christian.

The structured data includes Benedictus, Elisabeth and Otto, and records Christina's parents and her marriage to Georg. The first marriage to Otto is a separate union, not biological parentage.

**Source:** [von Kleist Family Archive, English translation, p. 123](https://www.v-kleist.com/FG/english/Dubberow_translation.pdf).

## 8. Genealogical scope: all well-founded connections

The research scope is not limited to legitimate descent or conventional nuclear families. Include, where supported:

- Biological parentage and descent, whether the child was born within or outside marriage.
- Acknowledged, recorded or otherwise well-supported non-marital paternity and maternity.
- Marriage, remarriage, partnerships and other documented unions, including overlapping unions.
- Adoption and legally or socially recognised adoptive parentage.
- Step-parent, foster, guardian and household relationships.
- Other documented connections such as maintenance, probate, inheritance, guardianship or acknowledged fatherhood.

Distinguish the relationship type and the evidence for each edge:

- **Biological:** asserted or supported biological parentage.
- **Marital/partner:** marriage or documented partnership; does not itself prove biological parentage.
- **Adoptive:** legal or documented adoptive parentage.
- **Step:** relationship through a parent's union, without implying biological descent.
- **Foster/guardian/household:** care or household connection, not biological descent unless separately evidenced.
- **Acknowledged/social/legal parent:** a person recognised as a parent in a source, with biological status separately stated if unknown or disputed.

Use calibrated statuses such as established, probable, possible/claimed, disputed and rejected. Do not infer illegitimacy from silence, stigma, a surname or a record gap. Do not turn a historical allegation or family story into established biological fact without evidence. Preserve contradictions and explain exactly what each source proves.

## 10. Export and data-model notes

tools/export_tree.py exports people and relationship assertions separately. It applies family_docs.json patches idempotently, reads parent and step references from the actual family.json schema, and reconciles GEDCOM family records as a second representation.

The exporter deliberately:
- Does not infer parent-child relationships from nested JSON structure.
- Does not convert a GEDCOM family membership or PEDI tag into biological descent.
- Does not infer biological paternity from words such as “paternity”, “declared” or “court”.
- Preserves explicit competing claims, sources and notes as edge-level assertions.
- Keeps person-record confidence separate from relationship evidence.
- Preserves sibof as a grouping/reference field rather than assuming full or half-sibling status.
- Does not infer marriage dates or legal status from a spouse pointer.

Run from the repository root:

```bash
python3 tools/export_tree.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_*.py'
```

The tests cover conflicting parentage claims, step-parenthood, GEDCOM adoption pedigree labels and idempotent document-patch merging. They should be run in the repository environment before treating the exporter as validated.

## 11. Publishing names of living relatives by consent

The normal public build anonymises living people and recent people without a death date. The opt-in workflow is documented in data/PUBLIC_CONSENT.md. The root Johannes is p1; his mother Vivi is p3.

Set public_consent=true only on individual records whose consent has been obtained. Decrypt and edit data/private_people.json locally, re-encrypt with data/private_tool.py, regenerate data/family.json, and review the generated public file before committing. Never share the password or commit the decrypted private file. Consent to publish a name does not automatically mean consent to publish addresses, contact details or sensitive personal notes.
