E. METHODOLOGICAL NOTE
====================================================

This expansion follows the editorial policy in leads/tree-research.md:
  • Only verified facts (with cited source) are entered as relationships.
  • Hypotheses, estimates, and unverified claims are NOT inserted as parent
    links. They remain research leads.
  • Medieval von Kleist material (Prissebur 1364, Klest de Densin 1289,
    Conradus/Bertoldus Clest 1248) is context-only and NOT added as direct
    ancestors — the generation-by-generation chain is not yet proven.
  • The von Kleist Tychow chain Reimer → Bartez → Andreas → Carsten → Bernd →
    Christian IS proven by VKA Dubberow p.122-145, which cites the feudal
    letters of 1477 (urk. 125), 1503, 1540, 1546, 1575, 1585, 1594, 1601,
    1605, 1608, 1618, 1621, 1622, 1665. This is a strong documentary chain.
