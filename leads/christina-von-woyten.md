# Christina von Woyten — dates and two Kleist marriages

**Person ID:** `p13899`  
**Current status:** identity and family context supported by published genealogy; birth and death dates not established.

## Why the tree has no birth or death dates

The sources currently attached to Christina identify her parents and marriages, but do not give a birth date, baptism, death date, burial, or an explicit age. The fact that her father Benedictus von Woyten is documented as living after 6 December 1584 is not a date for Christina and does not establish a reliable birth-year bound. The tree therefore correctly leaves both date fields empty.

## What the revised family history says

The 2025 revised edition of *Geschichte des Geschlechts v. Kleist — Tychow-Dubberower Linie* contains two relevant passages:

- **p. 143, biography II.55:** Georg von Kleist of Groß-Tychow (died after 1645) is said to have married Christina von Woyten, widow of Otto von Below of Pustamin, daughter of Benedictus von Woyten of Versin and Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz. The same biography says Georg left one daughter, Hedwig Maria. This is the passage supporting the current Christina–Georg II.55–Hedwig Maria family unit.
- **p. 36, biography II.44:** a different Georg von Kleist, associated with Crolow and dying in 1629, is said (citing von der Osten) to have married Christina Woyten of Versin, who bore him no children, and then Dorothea von Below, who bore two sons. The passage says the other family documents do not mention this first wife.

These are two distinct Georg von Kleists (II.44 and II.55), not one person with a spelling variant. The two Christina references may describe one woman in successive marriages, or two women with the same name and family origin. The source text does not explicitly resolve the identity. If it is one woman, the sequence would be Otto von Below, then Georg II.44 (who died 1629), then Georg II.55; that is a plausible research interpretation, not a proven chronology.

## Current data treatment

- Keep Christina's birth and death dates blank.
- Keep the current relationship to Georg II.55 and Hedwig Maria provisionally as recorded in the published genealogy, but expose the same-name / successive-marriage issue in the person note.
- Do not merge or create an additional Christina node until an independent source distinguishes the women or establishes the marriage sequence.
- Search for marriage settlements, inheritance/Lehn records, estate papers for Pustamin and Groß-Tychow, and parish records where surviving. The original sources cited by the 2025 volume (including von der Osten, pp. 48 and 156) are the next targets.
