## 2. Pedersen / Vester Starup line

Jens Nielsen Pedersen (`p10`) was born 14 April 1891 in Vester Starup and married Hulda Christensen on 11 June 1916 in Ringkøbing. At the 1925 census the family was in Silkeborg with daughters Karen Margrethe, Anna Elvira and Nelly; Liss was born there in 1931. His father Niels Pedersen (`p20`) was born 25 October 1861 in Vester Starup and married Ane Else Pedersen in 1889. The 1860 Vester Starup marriage of Jens Pedersen (`p40`) names his father as Peder Jensen and the bride Kirsten Marie Nielsen's father as Niels Jepsen. These parent references are recorded in the canonical tree but the earlier generation identities still need parish-record corroboration.

## 3. Hoven / Sønder Felding line

Hulda Christensen (`p11`) was born 25 September 1895 in Sønder Felding. Her father Mads Christensen/Kristensen (`p22`) was born 18 January 1852 in Hoven and married Karen Jensen in Grindsted in 1875. The Hoven birth entry identifies Mads's parents as Christen Andersen (`a44`) and Kirsten Christensdatter (`a45`).

The Hoven line has useful household evidence: Christen Andersen's mother Ane Kirstine Eskesdatter (`a89`) was a widow and owner of the Hoven mill in the 1834 census. Christen's household in 1860 included several children, including Mads. The mill ownership and widow household are useful collateral evidence for identity and chronology, but should not be treated as a substitute for the birth and marriage entries.

## 4. Pårup Melgaard / Assing / Sønder Felding — direct ancestral lead

Jens Peder Larsen (`a46`), born about 1812 in Assing, is a direct ancestor through Karen Jensen (`p23`), Hulda's mother. The current tree identifies his wife as **Kjersten Frederiksdatter** (`a47`), born 20 April 1822 in Sønder Felding, daughter of Frederik Jensen and Ane Thomasdatter. Their daughter Karen was born in Skarrild in 1852.

**Spouse discrepancy:** an uploaded expanded dossier says Jens Peder married Mette Pedersdatter in 1843. The existing tree and Horsbøl genealogy instead identify Kjersten Frederiksdatter, married 8 October 1843 in Sønder Felding. Horsbøl also lists their daughter Mette Marie, which may explain the dossier's mistaken Mette. The current working identification is Kjersten, but the original 1843 marriage entry must be inspected before treating the conflict as fully closed.
