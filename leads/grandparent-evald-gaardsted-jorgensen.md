## 2. Gaardsted / Vosnæsgaard / Rolsøgård

### Claus Gaardsted (`p72`) and Karen Christensdatter

**Status: strong sibling clue; parentage unresolved.**

The Rolsøgård G302 alphabetical probate index, under Niels Jensen Degn in Vrinners, probate no. 11, 14 May 1787, folio 39, records:

> “Arv i boet til afdødes stedbørn efter Christen Christensen Tohde? foged i Dejret (skifte 1764) til datter Karen Christensdatter LV: broder Claus Gaardsted forpagter på Vosnæsgård.”

The index identifies Karen Christensdatter as sister of Claus Gaardsted, tenant at Vosnæsgaard, and says he acted as her lavværge (legal guardian). It points to an earlier 1764 probate for bailiff Christen Christensen “Tohde?” in Dejret.

**Do not infer** that Tohde was Claus's or Karen's biological father. The wording refers to stepchildren and is not sufficiently clear to establish biological parentage. The original 1764 probate must be located and read. Also inspect the original 1787 folio 39 rather than relying solely on the alphabetical index.

Source: [Rolsøgård G302 index, 1778–1816](https://slaegt.dk/media/jnnnvuml/alfabetisk-navneliste-og-skifteuddrag-til-rols%C3%B8g%C3%A5rd-gods-g302-mols-herred-1778-1816-1818.pdf).

## 13. Clarification: Tohde was a stepfather, not an established biological father

The evidence-audit compilation parses the G302 clause as describing Karen Christensdatter and Claus Gaardsted as **stepchildren** of Christen Christensen “Tohde?” (foged in Dejret; probate 1764). This is consistent with the word `stedbørn`. The 1787 index therefore does **not** identify the biological father of Claus or Karen. It supports the sibling relationship and points to Tohde's earlier probate, which may name the biological parent(s). The exact wording must still be checked on the original folio 39.
