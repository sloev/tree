## Gaardsted — archive-series correction

The online alphabetical extract for Rolsøgård gods G302 identifies the relevant 1787 entry as:

- Niels Jensen Degn in Vrinners, probate no. 11, 14 May 1787, folio 39.
- The transcription names wife Maren Lauridsdatter, children and stepchildren, then reads: “Arv i boet til afdødes stedbørn efter Christen Christensen Tohde? foged i Dejret (skifte 1764) til datter Karen Christensdatter LV: broder Claus Gaardsted forpagter på Vosnæsgård.”

Source: https://slaegt.dk/media/jnnnvuml/alfabetisk-navneliste-og-skifteuddrag-til-rols%C3%B8g%C3%A5rd-gods-g302-mols-herred-1778-1816-1818.pdf

The extract is strong evidence that the 1787 estate mentions Karen Christensdatter as a stepchild/heir associated with an earlier probate for a bailiff named Christen Christensen “Tohde?” in Dejret, and that Claus Gaardsted is described as her brother and legal guardian, tenant at Vosnæsgård. It does **not** by itself resolve the uncertain surname reading or establish that Christen was Claus's father. The original 1787 folio still needs to be read.

A significant archive-series point: the G302 index is explicitly for Rolsøgård probate material **1778–1816 (1818)**. The cited 1764 probate therefore cannot simply be assumed to be in this same indexed series. Rigsarkivet's guidance says that before 1793 probate jurisdiction for non-freeholding rural residents depended on the estate/tenure: estate owners handled the probate of their tenants and employees; county-level probate series handled rural non-tenants. The correct 1764 archive creator must be identified from Christen's tenure/employer and the contemporary estate boundaries, rather than guessing “Rolsøgård 1764.”

Rigsarkivet guidance: https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/vejledning/skifter-og-testamenter/skiftearkivalier-kom-godt-i-gang/

Next step: inspect original G302 probate no.11, fol.39, then identify the 1764 probate's actual archive creator from the original reference and Dejret estate/tenancy context. Preserve “Tohde?” as uncertain until the original is read.
