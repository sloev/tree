I. BIOGRAPHICAL PROFILE & LIFE EVENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Name: Christina von Woyten
* Birth: Circa late 16th to early 17th century. Exact date and place are not 
  recorded in surviving primary documents. Given her father's post as the 
  princely Pomeranian captain (Hauptmann) of Bütow and the family's estate at 
  Versin (Vessin), she was likely born in Hinterpommern (Farther Pomerania), 
  within the modern-day Pomeranian Voivodeship of Poland.
* Status: Noblewoman (Adel) of the von Woyten family, an ancient Pomeranian 
  knightly lineage.
* Father: Benedictus (Benedict) von Woyten, Lord of Versin (Vessin) and 
  princely Pomeranian captain of Bütow. He died after 6 December 1584.
* Mother: Elisabeth von Stojentin of the House of Giesebitz.
* Siblings: She was one of four daughters. Her sister Regina von Woyten married 
  Gerson Kleist (a town councillor of Stolp). Other sisters included Sophia 
  (married Georg Schwave) and Elisabeth (married Philipp Jatzkow).

Marriages and Family:
1. First Husband: Otto von Below of Pustamin. She was widowed before her 
   second marriage.
2. Second Husband: Georg von Kleist of Groß Tychow (died after 1645). He was 
   a member of the Tychow-Dubberow line of the von Kleist family. 
   * Georg's feudal estates at Groß Tychow and Mandelatz passed to his 
     son-in-law, Colonel Christian von Kleist, upon his death.

Children:
* Hedwig Maria von Kleist: Daughter from her marriage to Georg von Kleist. 
  She married Colonel Christian von Kleist (II. 60), a relative.
* Note on Christian Casimir von Kleist (b. 1654): The user's original tree 
  listing suggests Christian Casimir was Christina's son. Genealogical 
  sources (such as the von Kleist family archives) clarify that Christian 
  Casimir was actually Christina's GRANDSON. He was the son of Hedwig Maria 
  von Kleist and Colonel Christian von Kleist. Christian Casimir was born in 
  1654 and died on 1 February 1722 in Oldenburg, Schleswig-Holstein.

II. EXPANDED ANCESTRAL TREE (PATERNAL & MATERNAL LINES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following lineage is reconstructed from the Zedler Universal Lexicon 
(article on the von Woyten family) and various Pomeranian noble genealogies. 
It traces the direct paternal line back to circa 1400 and the maternal line 
through the von Stojentin family of Giesebitz.

Generation 1 (Parents)
* Father: Benedictus (Benedict) von Woyten, Captain of Bütow (died after 1584)
* Mother: Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz

Generation 2 (Grandparents - Paternal)
* Grandfather: Michael von Woyten (Fähnrich in the service of Charles V)
* Great-Uncle: Martin von Woyten

Generation 3 (Great-Grandparents - Paternal)
* Great-Grandfather: Conrad von Woyten

Generation 4 (2x Great-Grandparents - Paternal)
* 2x Great-Grandfather: Conrad Pantaleon von Woyten

Generation 5 (3x Great-Grandparents - Paternal)
* 3x Great-Grandfather: Vandemar von Woyten

Generation 6 (4x Great-Grandparents - Paternal)
* 4x Great-Grandfather: Carl Woiten (flourished circa 1400). He is the 
  earliest recorded progenitor of the von Woyten family in Hinterpommern.

Maternal Line (von Stojentin of Giesebitz):
The von Stojentin family is an ancient Pomeranian noble house, first 
documented on 14 August 1341 in Stolp. The specific lineage of Christina's 
mother, Elisabeth von Stojentin, connects to the branch seated at Giesebitz 
(Izbica).
* Maternal Grandfather (probable): Hans (Johann) von Stojentin (1446–1517), 
  Lord of Gohren and Landvogt of Stolp. His parents were Peter von Stojentin 
  (circa 1400–1470) and Elisabeth von Lettow. 
* Maternal Great-Grandfather: Peter von Stojentin (circa 1400–1470).
* Maternal Great-Grandmother: Elisabeth von Lettow.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IV. RESEARCH NOTES & UNCERTAINTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The exact birth and death dates for Christina von Woyten remain 
  unrecorded. Her life is dated primarily through her marriages and the 
  documented timelines of her father and second husband.
* The placement of Hans von Stojentin (1446–1517) as the father of 
  Elisabeth von Stojentin is a strong genealogical hypothesis based on 
  estate holdings (Giesebitz) and contemporary peerage networks, but it is 
  not explicitly confirmed in a single primary record.
* The user's original tree confusingly listed Christian Casimir (b. 1654) 
  as Christina's son. Multiple sources (including the von Kleist archives) 
  confirm he was her grandson, the son of Hedwig Maria von Kleist and 
  Colonel Christian von Kleist.
