## 1. Verified additions made to the family tree

The English translation of the von Kleist family history, *History of the Family von Kleist – Tychow-Dubberow line*, page 123, states that:

- Georg von Kleist of Groß-Tychow (died after 1645) was married to Christina von Woyten.
- Christina was the widow of Otto von Below of Pustamin before her marriage to Georg.
- Christina was the daughter of Benedictus von Woyten of Versin, princely Pomeranian captain of Bütow, and Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz.
- Georg and Christina had one daughter, Hedwig Maria, who married Colonel Christian von Kleist (II. 60).
- Georg's feudal estates passed to his son-in-law Christian.

The existing tree already contained Georg, Christina and their daughter Hedvig/Hedwig Maria. The source data has now been extended with:
- Benedictus (Benedict) von Woyten, linked as Christina's father.
- Elisabeth von Stojentin, linked as Christina's mother.
- Otto von Below of Pustamin, recorded as Christina's first husband.
- A specific source entry for the von Kleist family archive translation.

**Primary source:** von Kleist Family Archive, [History of the Family von Kleist – Tychow-Dubberow line (English translation), p. 123](https://www.v-kleist.com/FG/english/Dubberow_translation.pdf).

## 2. Existing tree context

The source data already records:
- Georg von Kleist (II. 55), of Groß-Tychow, died after 1645.
- Christina von Woyten as Georg's wife.
- Hedwig Maria von Kleist as their daughter.
- Colonel Christian von Kleist (II. 60) as Hedwig Maria's husband.

The family archive passage supports those relationships. It also states that Georg left no legitimate heirs other than Hedwig Maria.

## 3. Research leads — not yet added as proven ancestry

### 3.1 Earlier von Woyten ancestry

The supplied dossier proposes this paternal sequence:

1. Michael von Woyten — described as an ensign in the service of Emperor Charles V.
2. Conrad von Woyten.
3. Conrad Pantaleon von Woyten.
4. Vandemar von Woyten.
5. Carl Woiten — said to have flourished around 1400.

It also names Martin von Woyten as a great-uncle of Benedictus.

These names and links require direct checking against the cited Zedler article and other contemporary records. The dossier does not provide page/column references or transcriptions sufficient to enter the parent-child links. They have therefore **not** been added to the tree.

### 3.2 von Stojentin maternal ancestry

The dossier suggests that Hans (Johann) von Stojentin (1446–1517), lord of Gohren and Landvogt of Stolp, may have been Elisabeth von Stojentin's father. It further names Peter von Stojentin (c. 1400–1470) and Elisabeth von Lettow as Hans's parents.

The dossier itself describes the connection to Elisabeth of Giesebitz as probable rather than demonstrated. No relationship has been added. The estate association with Giesebitz is not, by itself, proof of parentage.

### 3.3 Medieval von Kleist ancestry

The dossier proposes an early line beginning with:
- Klest de Densin, mentioned in 1289.
- Prissebur, reportedly mentioned in 1364.
- Jarßlaff, whose sons are reportedly mentioned in 1407.
- Henning, reportedly alive after 1435.
- Bisprow, reportedly enfeoffed in 1477 and dead around 1493.

It then refers to later Muttrin-Damen-line figures, including Pribislaff and Hans Jacob von Kleist (born 1654). The material mixes family numbering systems and does not establish a continuous, evidenced chain from the medieval names to the later individuals already in the tree. None of these people or links has been added.

**Research target:** inspect the original Muttrin-Damen family-history documents and the document references/footnotes cited by the family archive. Treat each generation separately; a family name or estate succession is not enough to establish biological descent.

### 3.4 Gedde / Gjedde

The dossier identifies:
- Ove Gjedde (1594–1660), son of Brostrup Gjedde and Dorthe Ulfeldt.
- Brostrup Gjedde (c. 1560–1614).

The existing tree already has an established Gedde officer lineage, but the supplied notes do not provide precise primary-record citations for these proposed links to the current branches. Do not attach them to the existing tree solely on the basis of the dossier. Verify against C. E. A. Schøller's *Stamtavle over Officersfamilien Gedde (Gjedde)* and contemporary records first.

Names and dates in these sections are not accompanied by individual record images, archive identifiers or sufficiently precise citations. They remain search leads, not verified additions.

## 4. Claims to resolve before further additions

- The supplied notes give inconsistent lists of Christina's sisters and use both Anna and Catharina. Do not add a sibling group until the underlying family record is checked.
- The proposed Hans von Stojentin → Elisabeth von Stojentin parent link is explicitly uncertain.
- The medieval von Kleist sequence needs source-by-source reconstruction, including checking whether similarly named Pribislaffs belong to different branches.
- Dates expressed as “mentioned in”, “flourished”, “alive after” or “died before” should be represented as dated events or date ranges, not converted into exact birth/death dates.
