Nordenbro-skiftet 1787: Christian Hansen og to ægteskaber

Kræver mere belæg

Claim

Et skifte fra 1787 i Nordenbro efter en Christian Hansen omtaler angiveligt to ægteskaber og flere børn. Det er ikke dokumenteret, at den afdøde er identisk med Christian Hansen (p114), født omkring 1806; skiftets børn kan muligvis tilhøre en senere generation.

Evidence

Dossieret beskriver skiftet som et endnu ikke tilknyttet spor og advarer specifikt mod at koble det til p114 alene på grund af navn og lokalitet.

Missing

Originalt skifte med skiftejurisdiktion, afdødes bopæl/alder, begge hustruers navne, børnenes navne og alder samt værger.

Next

Find skifteprotokollen for Magleby-Nordenbro gods omkring 1787. Søg efter en søn Christian født omkring 1806; hvis han ikke findes, registrér det negative resultat og luk identifikationssporet.

Note: Family-held Sydlangeland death list

Scan it at high resolution, preserve its title/creator/publication details and keep the original; the family notes suggest depositing a copy with Langelands Museum/local archive if the family agrees.

Note: 1787 Nordenbro probate — Christian Hansen

An open lead says a 1787 Nordenbro probate after Christian Hansen describes two marriages and several children in a local family. The extension suggests using it to test the identity of Christian Hansen (p114), born about 1806, who married Marie Madsdatter in 1829. The probate may concern a different Christian Hansen; the identity match is not established. Locate the correct probate jurisdiction and inspect the original for the deceased's age, spouse(s), children, residence and estate. Do not attach the family group to p114 based only on the name and locality.

Note: 16. Additional identity caution for the 1787 Nordenbro probate

The 1787 probate lead is explicitly described in the original extension as not yet connected to the tree. It concerns a deceased Christian Hansen in Nordenbro with two marriages and several children; one possible son of the right generation could be the Christian Hansen (p114) who married Marie Madsdatter in 1829. The 42-year gap is too long for the deceased Christian to be p114's father; the probate's children might be p114's generation or siblings. Read the probate for a son Christian born around 1806, the two wives' names and the guardian list. If no matching Christian appears, record the negative and close the lead. Source cited in the old extension: Magleby-Nordenbro gods probate series; a web lead was elgezira.dk/historie.html.
