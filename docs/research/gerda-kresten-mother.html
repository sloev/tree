Kresten Jørgensen: hvilken Marthe var hans mor?

Foreløbig hypotese

Claim

Familiearket angiver Christen Henriksen Jørgensen, født 3. november 1877 i Søndenbro, som mulig søn af Lars Thomsen Larsen og en kvinde ved navn Marthe Jørgensen. To mulige mødre er bevaret som kandidater; ingen er bevist.

Evidence

Dossieret nævner dåbsindekset LL:12-16292169 og folketællingerne 1880 og 1890, hvor Kresten skal være opvokset hos Jørgen Christensen og Karen Andersen. Det kan pege mod Marthe Jørgensen (født 1852), men er indirekte.

Missing

Den fulde dåbsindførsel med alder, bopæl, tjenestested og marginalnoter samt kandidat B's begravelse og tilgangsliste 1877.

Next

Læs dåbsbilledet først, og sammenhold derefter begravelse, tilgangsliste og kandidat A's folketælling 1921.

Note: 12. Kresten and the two possible mothers named Marthe Jørgensen

The family chart's lead is more specific than the earlier summary. It gives Christen Henriksen Jørgensen (g_kresten), born 3 November 1877 at Søndenbro, as a possible non-marital son of Lars Thomsen Larsen (p28), aged 18, and a woman recorded as Marthe Jørgensen (g_marthe). The mother's identity remains unresolved.

Two candidates are preserved: - Candidate A: Marthe Christine Jørgensen, born 25 January 1837 at Tryggelev; married Thomas Rasmussen Jespersen in 1861; had seven children and was alive in 1921. - Candidate B: Marthe Jørgensen, born 9 October 1852, daughter of Jørgen Christensen (Stougaard) and Karen Andersen; died 27 August 1878.

The extension leans toward candidate B because Kresten's foster parents in the 1880 and 1890 censuses were Jørgen Christensen and Karen Andersen, who were candidate B's parents. Candidate B's death in 1878 does not rule her out as Kresten's mother, but it means she could not have raised him after his birth. This is a hypothesis, not proof. A plausible age/household inference must not replace the original baptism.

Decisive checks, in order: 1. Read the full 1877 baptism entry cited as LL:12-16292169, not only the index. It reportedly names “Marthe Jørgensen” without a husband; inspect age, residence, employer (“tjener på”) and marginal notes. 2. Read candidate B's 27 August 1878 burial. If a surviving child is named, that is significant; absence of a child does not by itself disprove maternity. 3. Inspect the Magleby tilgangsliste for 1877 for the mother's residence and any child placement. 4. Search candidate A's 1921 census household for Kresten. 5. Test the MyHeritage claim that “Marthe Jørgensen fra Søndenbro” had three further children (Margrethe, Nielsine Marie and Otto August). These are not found in the Magleby register under her name in the existing notes; births after August 1878 cannot belong to candidate B.

Other open points: - Kresten's death has not been found. - Magleby church book 1916 reportedly names an unnamed child of Kresten and Marie Laurentine, born 15 January 1916 (g_kbarn). Read the full entry and identify whether the child was stillborn, unnamed at registration, or later named. - The prior exact-name web search and Strømming K-name index returned no match; this is a scoped negative search, not proof of non-existence.

Note: 18 November 2026 — Langeland death index for Marthe Christine Jørgensen

A new entry was found in Langeland Kommune Byhistorisk Arkiv's compiled index Døde og begravede på Langeland 1870–1899, compiled by Anders Gadegaard and last corrected 3 July 2025 (7,750 persons). On PDF line 4111 the index reads:

Jørgensen, Marthe Christine, tjenestepige, Søndenbro, Magleby, 26. + 27/8-1878. (MA)

The index header says Magleby parish (MA) is covered for 1870–1891. Source: Langeland death/burial index (PDF), accessed 18 November 2026.

Interpretation: this is a strong new possible identity match for candidate B: the indexed woman has the same reported death date (27 August 1878), the same parish and locality (Søndenbro, Magleby), and a compatible age/name. Candidate A, born in 1837, would have been about 41 and is a much poorer fit. However, candidate B's reported birth date is 9 October 1852, which would make her 25 years and 10 months old at death, while the index says 26. The one-year discrepancy must be checked against both original registers; neither derivative index should be treated as the original record.

What this source proves and does not prove: it proves that the local archive's index contains this entry. It does not prove that the indexed woman is candidate B, does not identify her parents, does not mention Kresten, and does not establish maternity or paternity. It is a finding aid, not a transcription of the original burial entry.

Next: inspect the original Magleby churchbook burial/death entry for 27 August 1878 and candidate B's 1852 birth/baptism entry; compare the exact name, age, residence, occupation and any marginal notes. Then return to the decisive 3 November 1877 baptism entry for Kresten and the 1877 Magleby tilgangsliste. Rigsarkivet's Arkivalieronline churchbook collection is the official access route, but the original images have not been inspected in this pass.
