Note: 7. Review of the comprehensive dossier and tree export

The uploaded complete_ancestry_dossier.txt and tree2.txt are useful indexes to the family tree, source references, image files, documents and collateral branches. They are secondary representations of the existing tree, not independent evidence for its relationships.

Important cautions retained from the export:

  • The tree2 snapshot reports 486 people, 14 generations, 593 source references and 157 people in the public diagram. These are snapshot metadata, not verified current database counts.
  • The export flags a duplicate identifier p13 for Else Gedde Jensen and Lovisa Larsson. The current structured family.json identifies p13 as Else Gedde Jensen; never merge records by ID alone when reconciling historical exports.
  • The Bornholm Lars Larsen (born 1771 in Rutsker, died 1854 in Allinge) is explicitly rejected as an erroneous automatic link. Do not reintroduce it.
  • Some Rousing-line ancestry is marked uncertain; preserve that uncertainty.
  • Disputed dates and unverified claims (including the reported suicide in the Larsen line) must remain disputed until checked against source records.
  • Living people are represented through encrypted records and anonymous public placeholders unless individually opted in to public naming.

This chain is substantially more developed than the short archive summary suggested, but the evidential quality is mixed: the 13th–14th-century parent links depend on later family records and a reconstruction; the 15th–17th-century line is supported by a denser series of fief, property, church and office records. Even where the family biography gives an explicit parent-child link, the cited original record must be checked to see whether it states parentage or merely identifies a person/estate. The expanded IDs are research records, not authority by themselves.
