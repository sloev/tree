Wilhelm Kroghs familie: forældre, bedsteforældre og søskende

Foreløbig hypotese

Claim

En sekundær slægtsdatabase fra Lokalhistorisk Arkiv Ulkebøl forbinder Wilhelm/Vilhelm Krogh (født 9. oktober 1908 i Tastrup/Torstrup ved Flensborg) med forældrene Detlef Krogh (1876–1949) og Kirstine Marie Jensen (1878–1945), samt en søskendeflok på fem. Det kan åbne en dokumenterbar Krogh-side af Lorenz' juridiske faderskabssag, men forbindelsen er endnu ikke bekræftet i originale tyske eller danske registre.

Evidence

Ulkebøl-registreringen angiver forældrene, søskende og flere livsdatoer. En anden side for Bothille Marie Hansen og en side for Peter Krogh gentager dele af samme familiegruppe. Dossieret har også et lokalt Nybøl-holdingspor for en ældre Detlef Krogh, men dette beviser ikke i sig selv slægtskabet.

Missing

Original tysk fødsels-/dåbsindførsel for Wilhelm i Adelby/Tastrup 1908; Detlef Kroghs vielse med Kirstine Marie Jensen i Flensborg; originale fødselsindførsler for søskende; kontrol af om den ældre Detlef Krogh i Nybøl er farfar. Stednavnet Tastrup/Torstrup skal læses i originalen.

Note: Wilhelm Krogh's origins and family

The extension gives a reported birth for Wilhelm Krogh (p12) of 9 October 1908 at a place read as either Tastrup or Vastrup, Kreis Flensburg. The place name must remain ambiguous until the 11 July 1932 Egernsund marginal note is reread at high magnification. Search the 1908 birth register at Kreisarchiv Schleswig-Flensburg, after identifying the correct Standesamt jurisdiction.

A possible brother is Detlef Krogh, born 8 May 1905, who reportedly married in Broager in 1929 and had daughters registered in Nybøl in 1936 and 1940. The extension also notes a Detlef Krogh (born 1849) and Botilde (born 1850) in the 1921 Nybøl census as possible older relatives. These are family-identification leads, not established sibling or grandparent links. The best next step is the 1905 Detlef's 1929 Broager marriage entry, which should name his father; compare it with Wilhelm's 1908 German birth entry.

For Sønderjylland after 1867, use German Standesamt civil registers and Kreisarchiv holdings as well as the relevant church books. The old extension identifies Broagerlands Lokalarkiv's teglværk personnel protocols as another possible source for Egernsund workers.

Note: Wilhelm Krogh: separate 1908 namesake candidate

The evidence audit notes a possible Carl Vilhelm Krogh, reportedly born 9 November 1908 in Tastrup/Flensburg and recorded in a 1925 Tandslet census, while this project's Wilhelm Krogh (p12) is recorded as born 9 October 1908 in Tastrup or Vastrup. Different given names and a one-month date difference mean they must not be merged. Request the 1908 Adelby church-book volume (book 2.3) from the Kirchenbuchamt Schleswig-Flensburg and compare exact parents and date with the Egernsund marginal note. The earlier candidate Detlef Krogh (born 1905) and the reported 1929 Broager marriage remain another route to the family.
