Christian Adam von Kleist: dåben i Vor Frelsers omkring 1705

Kræver mere belæg

Claim

Harbou angiver Christian Adam von Kleists fødsel 1. oktober 1705 og dåb i Vor Frelsers. Den præcise kirkebogsindførsel er endnu ikke læst, og ældre litteratur har en anden dato og fejl i faderskabet.

Evidence

Rigsarkivets udgivelsesmeddelelser viser, at klokkernes navneindeks 1704–1822 er offentliggjort online. Sognets inventar forbinder indeksbindet med dåbsbøgerne 15–19 og 36; der findes også en parallel ministerialbog. En supplerende genealogisk registrering hos Finn Holbek anfører 1. oktober 1705 i København og forældrene Curtz Ulrich von Kleist og Barbara Juliane von Kleist; registreringen henviser til Danmarks Adels Aarbog 1985–87, s. 722, og Gotha 1901, s. 473. Dette er sekundær henvisning og ikke en aflæst dåbsindførsel.

Missing

Det konkrete indeksopslag, side-/billednummer og original dåbsindførsel i både klokkerens og ministerens register. Finn Holbeks henviste DAA/Gotha-oplysninger er ikke kontrolleret direkte, og dåbsindførslen er stadig ikke identificeret.

Next

Find Christian/Christiern Adam i klokkerens navneindeks 1704–1822, følg boghenvisningen til dåbsindførslen, og sammenlign med præstens parallelle register; kontrollér samtidig DAA 1985–87 s. 722 og Gotha 1901 s. 473.

Note: 2026-11-16 — Vor Frelsers 1705 baptism: archive route confirmed, entry not yet read

A new source check clarifies the Vor Frelsers, Christianshavn, church-book structure. Rigsarkivet's 2025 Arkivalieronline release notes list the parish's Enesteministerialbog, content FVD 1686–1722. A separate published parish inventory explains that the minister and bell-ringer kept parallel registers before 1814. It identifies the minister's baptism books as F 1686–1813, volumes 1–5, and the bell-ringer's baptism books as F 1640–1813, volumes 12–19. The original baptism name index F 1704–1822 is volume 20 and points to the bell-ringer's baptism books 15–19 and 36.

Lookup sequence: (1) search the bell-ringer's name index, volume 20, for Christian Adam around 1 October 1705; (2) follow its reference into the indicated bell-ringer's baptism book; (3) independently inspect the minister's FVD 1686–1722 register; (4) compare dates, names, parents and contextual details, preserving any disagreement between the parallel entries.

This confirms the catalogue route and parallel-register structure, not the baptism entry itself. No image, page number or Christian Adam entry has yet been inspected. Harbou's date and parentage remain published-genealogy claims requiring more evidence. No canonical relationship or tree data is changed.

Sources: - Rigsarkivet, “Nyt på Arkivalieronline februar 2025”, p. 1 — lists Vor Frelsers Enesteministerialbog FVD 1686–1722. - Sogn nr. 47, Vor Frelsers Sogn, archival inventory — describes the parallel registers and volume 20 index route; this is an inventory aid, not the baptism record. - Rigsarkivet, guide to church books.

Next: inspect index volume 20 and follow its reference, then compare the minister's register. If the viewer does not expose the images, request the specific volumes from Rigsarkivet.

Closer reading of the parish inventory's wording adds an important access caveat: it says the original name registers are all available as copies in the reading room. Thus volume 20 is a precisely identified index, but I have not established that the index itself is digitized on Arkivalieronline. Note: Source audit — why the 1705 date and name still need the original entry (16 November 2026)

A further check of Harbou's printed footnote and the von Kleist family association's online correction clarifies the provenance of the date: - Harbou states Christian Adam was born 1 October 1705 and baptised at Vor Frelsers, but his footnote 28 only discusses the older Dansk biografisk Lexikon error (which made the maternal grandfather Christian Casimir his father) and the variant “Christian Adrian” in the German family history. It does not give a parish-register page, archive signature or direct citation for the baptism. - The family association's correction for III.466 says that an archive search in Danish records produced the date 1 October 1705 and the second name Adam rather than Adrian. It does not identify the specific register, image, page or archive signature. This is an additional reported archival finding, but still not a directly inspectable citation. - The older DBL article gives 3 October 1705 and the wrong father. The 1 October date is supported by Harbou and the family association's report of Danish archival research, but the discrepancy can only be settled by reading the original entry or the relevant archival index and its referenced book.

Accordingly, keep the date and parentage at “Kræver mere belæg”. Treat the family association's c.1960 archive-research statement as a provenance clue, not as a substitute for the underlying record. No canonical data changed.

Sources: - Harbou 1897, Christian Adam and footnote 28. - Familienverband derer v. Kleist, Ergänzungen zu III.466 — reports a Danish archive search but supplies no exact shelfmark or transcription.

Note: Update — the bell-ringer's 1704–1822 name index was released online (16 November 2026)

Correction to the access caveat above: the earlier inventory says the original name registers are available as reading-room copies, but Rigsarkivet's later Arkivalieronline release notices show that the relevant index was subsequently digitised/released online.

  • October 2025 release notice: Vor Frelsers Sogn, Navneregister til enesteministerialbog ført af klokkeren, 1640–1822, content “FØDTE 1704–1749 m.m.”
  • November 2025 release notice: same series, content “FØDTE 1704–1822” and a second index “FØDTE 1750–1842”.
  • The parish inventory identifies the 1704–1822 index as volume 20 and says it refers to the bell-ringer's baptism books 15–19 and 36.

This supersedes the previous assumption that volume 20 was not confirmed online. The online release is now documented, but the index's individual images have not yet been opened and Christian Adam has not yet been located in it. The next step is to open the Vor Frelsers name-index series in Arkivalieronline, inspect the index under Christian/Christiern/Christian Adam (and possible Adrian spelling), note the exact page/image and book reference, then follow that reference to the baptism entry. Compare the minister's FVD 1686–1722 book separately.

Status remains “Kræver mere belæg” until the index and original entry are actually read. No canonical family relationship changes.

Sources: - Rigsarkivet, “Nyt på Arkivalieronline oktober 2025”, p. 1 — first listed release of the Vor Frelsers bell-ringer name index, 1704–1749. - Rigsarkivet, “Nyt på Arkivalieronline november 2025”, p. 10 — lists the expanded 1704–1822 index. - Vor Frelsers parish archival inventory — identifies this as volume 20 and maps it to baptism books 15–19 and 36.
