Langø-familien: to børn med uafklaret videre liv

Kræver mere belæg

Claim

Familienotaterne angiver to yderligere børn af Marius Michelsen Christensen (p62) og Ane Marie Jørgensen (p63), hvis senere liv ikke er fulgt til ende: Christian Anton Christensen, født 1879 i Skrøbelev, og Christian Jørgen Christensen, født 1887 i København.

Evidence

Dossieret angiver konkrete fødselsdatoer og steder samt en senere bekræftelse i Humble i 1902 for Christian Jørgen. Det er et forskningsspor fra familie-/dossiermaterialet, ikke dokumentation for senere emigration eller dødsfald.

Missing

Originale dåbsindførsler, folketællinger efter 1880/1901, konfirmation/tilgangslister og eventuelle udrejsekontrakter eller passagerlister.

Note: 13. Langø household: two additional children to trace

The family notes list seven children of Marius Michelsen Christensen (p62) and Ane Marie Jørgensen (p63). Two remain open: - Christian Anton Christensen, born 17 June 1879 in Skrøbelev, before his parents' marriage. Search Skrøbelev baptism, 1880/1890 censuses and the lægdsrulle to trace residence and later life. - Christian Jørgen Christensen, born 1 December 1887 in Copenhagen, confirmed in Humble in 1902; absent from Danish censuses after 1901 and possibly an emigrant. Search the 1901 and 1911 censuses and Copenhagen Police emigration protocols 1902–1920 by name and birth parish.

The extension says Valdemar Emil Christensen took over Langø and was father of Lisbeth Skov, author of “En Langø-piges erindringer”. The memoir is a family-held private print. Scan it and preserve provenance; consider depositing a copy with Langelands Museum or the local archive with the family's permission.
