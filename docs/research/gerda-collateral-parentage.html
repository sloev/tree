Note: 2. Larsen / Magleby / Nordenbro line

Laurits Edvard Larsen (p14) was born 24 December 1898 in Nordenbro and died 28 July 1980. His father Lars Thomsen Larsen (p28) was born 1 November 1859 and died 22 December 1915. He married Hansine Petersen on 26 November 1881. The 1911 census shows their household with sons Aksel Peder, Hans Sigurd and Laurits Edvard. Four child deaths are listed in the family material, but the count “five children lost” remains unverified.

The tree traces Lars Thomsen Larsen through Lars Larsen (p56) to Lars Thomsen (p112) and Anne Katrine Jensdatter (p113). The MyHeritage-derived Bornholm Lars Larsen (born 1771 in Rutsker, died 1854 in Allinge) connection is explicitly rejected: the Magleby censuses 1834–1850 show the relevant Lars in the household of Lars Thomsen in Magleby. Do not reintroduce the Bornholm branch.
