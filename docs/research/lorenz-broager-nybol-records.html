The newly supplied lead/tree-research.md has been compared with the active dossiers. The following additional detail is retained here because it sharpens the von Woyten / von Stojentin / von Kleist research plan.

Christina von Woyten — dates absent; possible successive marriages / namesake issue: the 2025 revised German Tychow-Dubberower Linie (Familienverband derer v. Kleist, https://www.v-kleist.com/FG/Dubberow.pdf) gives two relevant biographies. On p. 143 (II.55), Georg von Kleist of Groß-Tychow (died after 1645) is said to have married Christina von Woyten, widow of Otto von Below of Pustamin, daughter of Benedictus von Woyten of Versin and Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz; Georg's only daughter was Hedwig Maria. This supports the current Christina–Georg II.55–Hedwig Maria unit. On p. 36 (II.44), a different Georg von Kleist of Crolow (died 1629) is said, citing von der Osten, to have married a Christina Woyten of Versin who bore him no children, and then Dorothea von Below, who bore two sons. These are two different Georgs. The two Christina references may describe one woman in successive marriages (Otto von Below → Georg II.44 → Georg II.55) or two women with the same name and family origin; the source does not resolve this. Keep this as an explicit identity question, not a settled chronology. No birth/baptism or death/burial date for Christina is supplied in either passage or the currently cited Harbou genealogy, so both date fields must remain blank. Her father's being documented after 6 December 1584 does not itself date her. Do not estimate her dates from relatives' lifespans or create a second Christina node without distinguishing evidence.

Earlier von Woyten ancestry — unverified Zedler lead: the supplied ledger proposes Michael von Woyten (ensign in Charles V's service) → Conrad von Woyten → Conrad Pantaleon von Woyten → Vandemar von Woyten → Carl Woiten (flourished c. 1400), and names Martin von Woyten as Benedictus's great-uncle. The supplied material gives no precise Zedler page/column or underlying source. Treat every generation as a separate research lead, not as an established chain. Next inspect Grosses vollständiges Universal-Lexicon, vol. 59, article “Woyden, Woyten”, capture exact page/column and wording, then follow its citations.

Further Stojentin hypothesis: the ledger suggests Hans (Johann) von Stojentin (1446–1517), lord of Gohren and Landvogt of Stolp, as a possible father of Elisabeth von Stojentin of Giesebitz; it further proposes Peter von Stojentin (c. 1400–1470) and Elisabeth von Lettow as Hans's parents. This remains unproven. An estate association or shared family name is not parentage evidence.

Early Muttrin / medieval von Kleist names: the ledger also lists Klest de Densin (mentioned 1289), Prissebur (1364), Jarßlaff and sons (reportedly mentioned 1407), Henning (alive after 1435), and Bisprow (enfeoffed 1477, died c. 1493). The existing exp020–exp042 cautions remain controlling: distinguish contemporary charter statements from later family-genealogy reconstructions, keep numbering systems separate, and do not bridge generations without the cited document.

Gjedde versus Gedde: the supplied ledger names admiral Ove Gjedde (1594–1660), son of Brostrup Gjedde and Dorthe Ulfeldt, and Brostrup Gjedde (c. 1560–1614). Note: Detlef Krogh in the 1851 Nybøl property list

The local-history booklet Fra Nybøl sogn, no. 3 (1981), contains a list titled “Fortegnelse over bol, kådnere og inderste i Nybøl 1851” (contents page 71; printed list on p. 71). Under “Kådnere - Husmandssteder” it lists “32. Detlef Krogh - Fra 1883 kun indersted.” The same list separately labels the next category “Inderste - Husejere.” The booklet explains that the list is keyed to a town map and that some numbered sites had been moved or had earlier uses. This is a specific place/holding lead for a Detlef Krogh in Nybøl, not a birth, family, or identity record. The phrase “Fra 1883 kun indersted” appears to describe a change in the status/use of the site from 1883; it must not be read as proof that Detlef himself lived there continuously from 1851 to 1883, nor as a date of birth or death. No link has yet been demonstrated between this Detlef and the proposed Wilhelm father Detlef Krogh (b. 1876), the proposed brother Detlef (b. 1905), or the older Detlef Krogh senior (c. 1849–1931). The property number and map are useful targets for land, census, and local address cross-checking.

Source: Peter Petersen, “Fortegnelse over bol, kådnere og inderste i Nybøl 1851,” in Fra Nybøl sogn, no. 3 (Hjemstavnsforeningen for Nybøl Sogn, 1981), p. 71; digitised copy at https://slaegtsbibliotek.dk/2025/953243.pdf (PDF printed page 71; extracted text lines 2142–2193). The publication's contents and introductory note identify this as a 1981 local-history volume and describe the register as map-based.

Note: Adelby 1908 baptism: catalogue discrepancy and access route

The Landeskirchliches Archiv's Adelby church-book overview describes a baptism register “Taufen 1882–1949,” book no. 2.3, and specifically notes entries concerning the naming of illegitimate children. This is the right register series to ask about for a claimed Wilhelm Krogh birth on 9 October 1908. However, the currently visible Archion catalogue entry for Adelby “Taufen 1882–1907” (local signature Adelby_2.3) only advertises digitised coverage through 1907; it does not expose a 1908 volume/entry in the public catalogue. The register overview and online catalogue therefore do not align on the displayed end date. I have not inspected the original 1908 entry and cannot yet confirm whether 9 October is a birth date or baptism date, or whether the parents are recorded as Detlef Krogh and Kirstine Marie Jensen.

Sources: - Landeskirchliches Archiv, Kirchenbuchübersicht 1 Schleswig-Flensburg, Adelby entry: https://www.archiv-nordkirche.de/files/landeskirchliches_archiv/Kirchenbuchuebersichten/Kirchenbuch%C3%BCbersichten/Kirchenbuchuebersicht_1_Schleswig-Flensburg.pdf - Archion, Adelby, “Taufen 1882–1907,” local signature Adelby_2.3: https://www.archion.de/de/alle-archive/schleswig-holstein/landeskirchliches-archiv-der-evangelisch-lutherischen-kirche-in-norddeutschland/kirchenkreis-schleswig-flensburg/adelby/273637
