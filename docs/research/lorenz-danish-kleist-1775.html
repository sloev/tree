Frederik Christian von Kleist: dåb i Kolding 1775 og identitet

Kræver mere belæg

Claim

Selmer beskriver en Frederik Christian af Kleist født 3. januar 1775 i Kolding, mens Harbou angiver 8. januar 1775 for Frederik Christian III.727. Identiteten er stærkt sandsynlig, men fødselsdatoen og den præcise dåbsindførsel er ikke afgjort.

Evidence

Den samtidige nekrolog hos Selmer og Harbous slægtshistorie matcher på forældre og dødsdato 21. maj 1848. Kolding kirkebøger C 469-3 og C 469-7 dækker januar 1775 og er udpeget som direkte kontrolkilder.

Missing

Original dåbsindførsel med fødsels-/dåbsdato, forældre, bopæl og faddere; afklaring af om Selmers 3. januar er fødselsdato eller en afvigende oplysning.

Note: Christian Adam III.466's actual descendants in Harbou

Harbou gives Christian Adam III.466 and Sophie Rosenkrantz five children. Their eldest surviving son was Christian Frederik III.601, born 25 August 1743, died at Bredsted 12 July 1799; he married Anna Margrethe Schubart on 3 December 1770. Harbou lists four sons and four daughters from that marriage:

  • Christine Sophie, born 28 August 1771, died 31 March 1862; married Edvard Snedorph Hammer in 1795.
  • Adam Ludvig Vilhelm (III.725), born 16 October 1772, died 6 October 1851 at Viborggaard.
  • Valentin Ulrik (III.726), born 31 December 1773, died 23 July 1827; married Anna Matthea Bierg on 30 November 1811. Their children include Margrethe Christine Elisabeth (1813), a daughter who died in infancy (1814), Frederik Christian III.853 (1815–1892), Holgerine Henriette Caroline (1817–1845), Christian Conrad Sophus (1820–1880), Aadamine Frederikke Christiane (1822–1874), Riise Sophie Emilie (1824–1864), and Johan Eduard Valentin (1825).
  • Frederik Christian (III.727), born 8 January 1775, died 21 May 1848. This is a plausible identification behind the supplied “Christian Frederik Vilhelm” (1774–1848), but Harbou's name and birth date differ. It is a candidate identity, not a proven match; compare the supplied source and primary records before merging.
  • Carl Gottlieb (III.728), born 15 January 1778, died 26 July 1849.
  • Juliane Frederikke Margrethe, born 2 December 1781, died 22 May 1861; married Norwegian major-general Nicolai Wilhelm Gedde in 1811.
  • Frederikke Louise Dorthea, born 5 January 1786, died 24 January 1870; married Ove Samuel Gedde in 1806.
  • Henriette Louise, born 30 November 1795, died 12 April 1827; married Johan Adolf von der Recke in 1813.

Harbou also lists Emil Christian (III.601a), baptized 25 June 1745 and died 13 September 1745; Carl Christian (III.601b), baptized 9 June 1749 and died 15 August 1750; and an unnamed daughter who died young in 1753. The 1743–1799 Christian Frederik is therefore not to be confused with his younger brother(s) or with later descendants.

Note: What this changes about the supplied pedigree

The 1774–1848 entry may be a distorted rendering of Harbou's Frederik Christian III.727 (1775–1848), a son of Christian Frederik III.601, rather than a distinct “Christian Frederik Vilhelm.” If the supplied source's individual has the same spouse, children, military/civil career or place of death, that would help establish the match. At present, the identity remains a hypothesis because the forename and birth year do not agree.

The claimed Sophie Frederikke (1776–1851) is not identified in Harbou's list of Christian Frederik III.601's children. It could be a different individual, a spouse, or a conflation; no relationship is added. Likewise, the 1742/43–1815 “Frederik Carl Emil” is not one of the children Harbou lists for Christian Adam III.466. It remains unplaced.

The earlier Copenhagen branch correction stands: the 1813–1825 people named above are children of Valentin Ulrik III.726 and Anna Matthea Bierg, not of the supplied 1770s couple. Harbou's source gives Margrethe's birth as 25 August 1813, Frederik Christian III.853's birth as 8 October 1815, and Holgerine's marriage to Hans Lindholm as 13 October 1838.

Note: What the cited source actually says

H. W. Harbou, “Slægten von Kleist i Danmark,” Personalhistorisk Tidsskrift (1897), in the entry for Christian Adam III.466 and his children, gives III.727's name, birth date, and military progression (page, cadet, officer in the Sjællandske Jægerkorps, company commander, major, and finally brevet/character lieutenant-colonel), ending only with “† 21/5 1848.” The entry does not name a death place or burial place. Harbou's footnote 36 points to H. P. Selmer, Nekrologiske Samlinger, vol. II. This is the immediate next secondary source to inspect for a fuller death notice and possible residence/place of death. The earlier suggestion that he died at Bredsted is not established by this passage and should not be repeated as fact.

Harbou separately states that III.601 (Christian Frederik, born 1743) owned property near Bredsted and died there in 1799. That Bredsted location belongs to III.601, not III.727; avoid transferring it to his younger brother.

Because Harbou does not supply a place of death, both parish routes remain conditional rather than proof that either was his parish.

Note: Samtidig nekrolog — biografisk identifikation

H. P. Selmer, Nekrologiske Samlinger, 1848–49, s. 68, omtaler “Oberstløjtnant Frederik Christian af Kleist”. Han beskrives som: - født 3. januar 1775 i Kolding; - søn af kammerherre Christian Frederik af Kleist og Anne Margrethe født Schubarth; - først page hos Christian VII, derefter landkadet og omkring 1792/1793 fændrik ved Sjællandske Jægerkorps; - senere kaptajn og kompagnichef; - afskediget 1. marts 1816 på grund af svagelighed med pension som major, senere med oberstløjtnants afsked; - død 21. maj 1848.

Selmers dødsfortegnelse i samme bind, s. 114, anfører igen oberstløjtnant Frederik Christian af Kleist, død efter mange lidelser, 73 år gammel. Kombinationen af navn, rang, dødsdato, alder, forældre og militær karriere gør identifikationen med Harbous III.727 meget stærk. Den bekræfter dog ikke den alternative identifikation med den fremlagte “Christian Frederik Vilhelm” (1774–1848).

Note: Begravelse

Brejls oversigt over private personarkiver omtaler et 100-årigt gravbrev dateret 27. maj 1848 for et gravsted på Garnisons Kirkegård uden Østerport. Heri er oberstløjtnant Frederik Christian von Kleist registreret som nedsat 26. maj 1848. Det giver en konkret begravelsesdato og kirkegård, men oversigten er en sekundær registrering af et gravbrev — ikke selve gravbrevet eller kirkegårdens originale begravelsesprotokol. Den dokumenterer derfor begravelsesstedet bedre end dødsstedet; den beviser ikke, at han døde i København.

Note: Follow-up: Kolding Stadsarkivs afskrifter som vej til dåben (16. november 2026)

En konkret, lokal kildevej er identificeret til at afgøre fødselsdatokonflikten mellem Selmers 3. januar og Harbous 8. januar 1775.

Kolding Stadsarkiv oplyser, at lokalhistorikeren Ingrid Bruuns arkiv indeholder afskrifter af kirkebøger fra Kolding Kommune for perioden 1635–1881. Afskrifterne er digitaliseret og frit tilgængelige via en søgeportal. Det er et nyttigt søgehjælpemiddel til at lokalisere den relevante indførsel og kontrollere navnevarianter, men afskriften bør sammenholdes med den originale kirkebog, før dato og forældre anses for endeligt fastslået.

Kolding Stadsarkivs side om slægtsforskning linker direkte til Ingrid Bruuns afskrifter: https://koldingstadsarkiv.dk/brug-arkivet/slaegtsforskning

Status: Søgeportalen (salldata.dk/ibp/index.php) gav timeout under denne undersøgelse. Der er derfor endnu ikke fundet eller læst en konkret dåbsindførsel for Frederik Christian. Dette er en ny søgevej, ikke ny persondokumentation.

Note: Follow-up: independent catalogue evidence for Kolding's 1775 baptism search (16 November 2026)

A further check of Kolding's historical church-book holdings identifies a more specific archival route, but still does not reveal the baptism entry itself.

A published archival overview of Kolding's older church books lists the parish priest's register C 469-3 for 1751–1775 and C 469-4 for 1776–1814. The same overview notes a supplementary register of births and baptisms for 1764–1800, held in the parish archive under C 469-74, begun in 1776 under an order of 29 April 1776 (Royal Rentekammer letter of 22 June). This supplementary register may therefore be especially relevant for checking a child born in January 1775 only if its coverage or retrospective entries extend earlier than its stated start; it must not be assumed to include him. The main 1751–1775 register C 469-3 is the direct chronological target for January 1775.

Kolding Stadsarkiv confirms that Ingrid Bruun's transcriptions cover Kolding-area church books from 1635 to 1881 and are searchable online. Its broader source-collections page also describes the transcription collection. The transcriptions are a finding aid, not a substitute for checking the original register image. Rigsarkivet's guidance says that pre-1814 parish books are generally chronological enesteministerialbøger, so the original search should follow the chronological register for the relevant parish rather than presume a modern tabular birth register.

Note: Correction and refinement: the actual Kolding baptism books (16 November 2026)

The archival signature in the previous follow-up needs correction. Paul G. Ørberg's article “Koldings gamle kirkebøger” in Vejle Amts Årbog 1982, pp. 89–101, gives a more detailed inventory than the shorthand used earlier:

  • C 469-3, the parish priest's church book, covers 1751–1815 and includes baptisms 1751–1815. This is the principal chronological register for the January 1775 baptism.
  • C 469-7, the parish clerk's church book, covers 1723–1779 and includes baptisms 1723–1776. This is a second contemporary register that also covers January 1775.
  • C 469-8, the parish clerk's church book, covers 1774–1815, but its listed legitimate-baptism section begins in 1780 and illegitimate baptisms in 1777. It is therefore not the direct baptism volume for a legitimate child in January 1775.
  • C 469-74 is a supplementary list of births and baptisms 1764–1800, begun in 1776. It is not a safe primary target for January 1775.

Ørberg explicitly cautions that both church books should be used in historical investigations, since entries, dates, name forms and marginal notes may differ. This makes the comparison of C 469-3 and C 469-7 the correct next step. Rigsarkivet's March 2026 Arkivalieronline update independently lists Kolding Sankt Nikolaj's scanned enesteministerialbog content as FVD 1723–1779 and FVD 1774–1815. The latter catalogue grouping should not be confused with Ørberg's older volume-level description of C 469-8's baptism sub-series.
