Note: 14. Emigration: precise protocol references

The earlier general leads can now be made more actionable: - Iver Peder Holgersen → Fresno: Copenhagen Police emigration contract no. 4443, dated 8 September 1910. The open-web search for the name and Fresno returned nothing relevant; continue from the contract number to passenger records, California directories/census, naturalization and death records. - Olaf Gedde → Shanghai: contract no. 3534, dated 11 July 1904; see Lorenz dossier because Olaf is Paula Gedde's brother. - Emil Gedde → Argentina: the Eldorado colony has two photographs already cited as B9886 and B9790; search the colony register and Danish-Argentine congregation records; see Lorenz dossier. - Jørgen Christensen and Bodil Margrethe Clausdatter: nine children are reported; the family says seven emigrated to America, the eldest son and youngest daughter stayed in Denmark, and the parents followed then returned. Five children are already named in the tree with baptism citations. Search emigration protocols parish-first (Magleby, Skrøbelev, Humble, Nordenbro), then US censuses and Danish-American church registers. Do not assume all nine travelled together.

The Copenhagen police protocols cover people who bought a contract ticket through an emigration agent; independent travellers may be missing. A null protocol search is not proof that someone stayed in Denmark.
