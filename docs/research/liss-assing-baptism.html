Note: 4. Pårup Melgaard / Assing / Sønder Felding — direct ancestral lead

Jens Peder Larsen (a46), born about 1812 in Assing, is a direct ancestor through Karen Jensen (p23), Hulda's mother. The current tree identifies his wife as Kjersten Frederiksdatter (a47), born 20 April 1822 in Sønder Felding, daughter of Frederik Jensen and Ane Thomasdatter. Their daughter Karen was born in Skarrild in 1852.

Spouse discrepancy: an uploaded expanded dossier says Jens Peder married Mette Pedersdatter in 1843. The existing tree and Horsbøl genealogy instead identify Kjersten Frederiksdatter, married 8 October 1843 in Sønder Felding. Horsbøl also lists their daughter Mette Marie, which may explain the dossier's mistaken Mette. The current working identification is Kjersten, but the original 1843 marriage entry must be inspected before treating the conflict as fully closed.
