## The key link: how Evald connects to Aage Jørgensen and Johanne Marie Gaardsted 🔗

Evald's birth record from 1922 is newer than anything in the public index, so I had to infer his parents. Four facts point to the same couple:
1. **The 1921 census for Kolind** (record `25-1032484…86`) shows widow **Rasmine (Gaardsted)**, b. 1855, with her unmarried daughter **Johanne Marie Gaardsted**, b. 1879, and **Aage Jørgensen**, b. 17 Oct 1890 in Slagelse, listed as a servant in the same house.
2. Aage's full name is **Aage *Evald* Oskar Jørgensen**. Your grandfather is **Evald *Johannes* Gaardsted-Jørgensen**: Evald from Aage, Johannes from Johanne, and Gaardsted from her family.
3. Evald was born in **Kolind**, the same village, 18 months later.
4. No other Gaardsted family in the area has a Jørgensen connection.

**To confirm it**, look up Kolind parish church book, births 1922 (Djurs Sønder Herred), on [Arkivalieronline](https://www.sa.dk/ao/). It's free, but you have to log in. One caution: Johanne Marie would have been 43 when Evald was born. That's unusual but possible.

### Generation 4 – great-great-grandparents
**Jørgen Anton Jørgensen** – b. 6 Oct 1857, Vor Frue parish, Copenhagen (`12-13117126`; later records say 1858). Confirmed in St. Paul's parish in 1872. Typographer in Copenhagen in 1880 and 1885, then at *Sorø Amts Bogtrykkeri* in Slagelse in 1901. Married **Lovisa Larsson** on 31 Jan 1886 in Sankt Johannes, Copenhagen (`13-4147709`). Widowed in 1910 and died 4 Aug 1914 in Slagelse (`11-230038`).

**Lovisa "Louise" Larsson** – b. 28 Jun 1862 in Malmö, Sweden. She was a housemaid for a merchant's family in Copenhagen in 1885 and died 12 Oct 1910 in Slagelse (`11-231267`).
