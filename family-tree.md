## What's new this round

- **Evald's parents are confirmed.** His own birth entry in the Kolind church book (1922, boys no. 6) gives:
  - his father, *gaardbestyrer* (farm manager) **Aage Evald Oskar Jørgensen**, b. 17 Oct 1890;
  - his mother, **Jensine Mariane Magdalene Gaardsted**, b. 6 Jun 1894. This corrects my earlier guess: she is the younger sister of Johanne Marie.
  - His godparents were his grandmother Rasmine and his uncles Martin Marinus and Axel Gaardsted.
  - A margin note records the 1985 name change to Gaardsted-Jørgensen.
  - Evald was born on **20 Sep 1922**. The 20 Aug date found online is wrong.
- **Evald's childhood.** In 1925 he was at Gjellerup poorhouse near Herning, which his father managed. His sister was Ester Kristine (b. 1924). The family moved to **Hadsten** in 1932.
- **Jochum Gaardsted's baptism is found.** The Skødstrup church book, 2 Oct 1792, says he was born 23 Sep 1792 at **Vosnæsgaard manor**. His parents were *forpagter* (tenant of the manor) **Claus Gaardsted** and **Kirsten Marie Jørgensdatter**. His godparents were officers' wives and gentry. Claus later bought the **Bogensholm** estate on Mols (1796/97) and died in 1811.
- **Liss.** Evald's archive in Hadsten Lokalarkiv gives her birth date, 18 Apr 1931. She trained as a parish clerk in Hune and died in 2002.
- **Archives to ask for copies:**
  - Hadsten Lokalarkiv A37: Evald's papers, including **his own "Slægtstavle"** (family tree).
  - Midtdjurs Lokalhistoriske Arkiv A847: Johanne and Sofie Gaardsted's memoirs of Petersminde, Kolind.

## Still open
- **Lorenz Frederiksen and Gerda.** I need birth dates and places. An illegitimate child is often baptised under the mother's surname, and the birth entry names the mother and sometimes the alleged father.
- **Liss's parents.** Her birth parish would unlock the Pedersen line.
- **Claus Gaardsted's origins.** He came to Vosnæsgaard after 1784, and his first marriage was elsewhere.
- **Lovisa Larsson.** Her line continues in Swedish records (Malmö, 1862; father Niels Larsen).
- **Niels Jørgensen's birthplace.** It is written "Hove/Høje, Svendborg amt", and I couldn't identify that parish.
